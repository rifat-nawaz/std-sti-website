## Activities with no realistic risk

Kissing, sharing cups or utensils, spitting, coughing, hugging, sharing a toothbrush (though not recommended for other reasons).

## Negligible risk

| Activity | Why |
|---|---|
| Biting | Saliva doesn't carry enough virus; blood exposure is rare |
| Kissing with bleeding gums | Only theoretical, extremely rare cases |

If you're anxious after a possible exposure, see [HIV anxiety](/hiv/anxiety/).
