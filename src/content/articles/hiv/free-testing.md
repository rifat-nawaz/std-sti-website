## Where to get free testing

| Option | Notes |
|---|---|
| Health department | Often walk-in, free |
| Community health centers | Sliding scale or free |
| LGBTQ+ and AIDS service organizations | Often free rapid tests |
| Mailed self-test programs | Free kits in many areas |
| Testing events | Pride events, National HIV Testing Day |

Find sites at [GetTested](https://gettested.cdc.gov/).
