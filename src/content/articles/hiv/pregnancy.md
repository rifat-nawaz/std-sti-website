## Steps that protect the baby

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| Early pregnancy | HIV testing; start or continue care |
| During pregnancy | Regular viral load checks |
| Delivery | Planned based on viral load |
| After birth | Preventive care and testing for the baby |
| Feeding | Plan agreed with your care team |

See [having children with HIV](/hiv/having-children/).
