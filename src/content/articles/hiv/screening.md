## Who and how often

| Who | How often |
|---|---|
| Everyone 15 to 65 | At least once |
| Pregnant people | Every pregnancy |
| People with new or multiple partners | At least yearly |
| Gay and bisexual men at higher risk | Every 3 to 6 months |
| People who inject drugs | At least yearly |
| Anyone diagnosed with another STD | Now |
