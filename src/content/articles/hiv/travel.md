## Travel checklist

- Enough medicine for the trip plus extra days
- Original pharmacy labels
- A letter from your clinician (no need to state the diagnosis if you prefer)
- Travel insurance that covers HIV care
- Clinic contacts at your destination

## Work visas

Some countries test for HIV for work or residence visas. Rules change; check with the embassy or employer.
