## Side by side

| | HIV | AIDS |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | The virus | Late stage of untreated HIV |
| CD4 count | Can be normal | Below 200 |
| Diagnosis | Positive HIV test | HIV plus low CD4 or certain infections |
| Outlook with care | Near-normal lifespan | Immune system can still recover |
