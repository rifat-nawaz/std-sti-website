## Prevention toolkit

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [PrEP](/hiv/prep/) | About 99% lower risk from sex |
| Condoms | Lower HIV and STD risk |
| U=U | Undetectable partners don't transmit |
| [PEP](/hiv/pep/) | Emergency prevention within 72 hours |
| Regular testing | Finds infections early |
