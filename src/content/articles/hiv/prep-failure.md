## Why PrEP sometimes fails

| Reason | How common |
|---|---|
| Missed doses or appointments | The most common reason |
| HIV already present when PrEP started | Uncommon; why testing comes first |
| Resistant HIV | Very rare |

## How to stay protected

- Follow your PrEP plan exactly.
- Set reminders for doses and appointments.
- Keep up with HIV and STD tests.
- Talk to your clinician before stopping or changing PrEP.

## After a lapse

If you missed PrEP and had a possible exposure in the last 72 hours, ask about [PEP](/hiv/pep/) today.
