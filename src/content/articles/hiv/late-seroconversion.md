## When longer follow-up may be advised

- After taking PEP
- Starting PrEP during an undiagnosed infection
- Certain immune conditions

## If you keep retesting

Repeated negative tests after the window period mean you don't have HIV. See [HIV anxiety](/hiv/anxiety/).
