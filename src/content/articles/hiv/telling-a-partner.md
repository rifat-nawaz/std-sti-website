## Sample words

> "There's something important I want to share. I'm living with HIV. I'm on treatment and undetectable, which means I can't pass it on through sex. I'm happy to answer any questions."

## Past partners

Health department partner services can tell them they may have been exposed without naming you.

## Dating apps

Some people list their status and "U=U" on profiles; others share before meeting. Both are valid.

See [HIV disclosure laws](/hiv/disclosure-laws/) and [dating with HIV](/hiv/dating/).
