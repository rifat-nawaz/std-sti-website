## Reading the result

| What you see | Result |
|---|---|
| Line at C only | Non-reactive (negative) |
| Lines at C and T (any strength) | Reactive: get a lab test |
| No line at C | Invalid: retest |

## Accuracy

Antibody tests take longer to detect HIV than lab antigen/antibody tests. Test again at 90 days if your first test was earlier.

See [HIV window period](/hiv/window-period/).
