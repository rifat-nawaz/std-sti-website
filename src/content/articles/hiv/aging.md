## Checkups to ask about

| Area | Checks |
|---|---|
| Heart | Blood pressure, cholesterol, diabetes |
| Kidneys | Blood and urine tests |
| Bones | Bone density scan when advised |
| Cancer | Age-appropriate screening, including anal cancer for some |
| Brain and mood | Memory and depression screening |

## Healthy habits

Stop smoking, stay active, eat well, limit alcohol, and stay in care.
