## Side by side

| | PrEP | PEP |
|---|---|---|
| When | Before exposure | Within 72 hours after |
| Length | Ongoing | About 4 weeks |
| Who | People at ongoing risk | Anyone with a recent possible exposure |
| Where | Clinician, clinic, telehealth | ER, urgent care, clinic |
