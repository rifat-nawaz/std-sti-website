## What affects life expectancy

| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Early diagnosis | Better outcomes |
| Staying in care, undetectable | Near-normal lifespan |
| Late diagnosis, low CD4 | Higher risks |
| Smoking, heavy drinking | Reduces life expectancy |
| Other conditions (hepatitis, heart disease) | Need management |
