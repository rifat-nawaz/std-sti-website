## What to say

> "Thank you for trusting me. How can I help?"

## What to avoid

- Blame or questions about how it happened
- Treating them as fragile or contagious in daily life
- Sharing their diagnosis

## Practical help

Rides to appointments, help with paperwork, reminders, or simply spending time together.

See [starting HIV care](/hiv/treatment/).
