## Options for staying negative

| Option | Protection |
|---|---|
| Partner undetectable for 6+ months | No sexual transmission |
| PrEP for you | About 99% lower risk from sex |
| Condoms | Much lower risk |
| All combined | Maximum reassurance |

## Testing schedule

At least yearly, or every few months if on PrEP.
