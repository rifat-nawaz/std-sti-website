## What laws may cover

- Disclosure before sex or needle sharing
- Penalties for exposure, sometimes regardless of transmission risk
- Enhanced penalties in some situations

## Recent reforms

Several states have updated laws to reflect current science, such as the role of viral suppression and condoms.

## Where to get help

Local legal aid, HIV service organizations and national HIV legal advocacy groups. See the CDC's [state HIV laws](https://www.cdc.gov/hiv/policies/law/states/).

This page is general information, not legal advice.
