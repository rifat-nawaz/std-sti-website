## Your options

| Option | Who it's for |
|---|---|
| Employer or Marketplace insurance | Most working adults; can't be denied for HIV |
| Medicaid | People with low income (rules vary by state) |
| Medicare | People 65+ or with qualifying disabilities |
| Ryan White HIV/AIDS Program | People with low income or gaps in coverage |
| ADAP (state program) | Helps pay for HIV medicine and sometimes premiums |
| Manufacturer assistance | Copay help or free medicine for eligible people |

## How to get help

1. Ask your HIV clinic for a **case manager** or **benefits navigator**.
2. Bring proof of income, residency and any insurance.
3. Re-certify on time each year so coverage doesn't lapse.

## Choosing a health plan

When choosing a plan, check that your HIV clinic and clinician are in network and that your prescribed medicines are covered. A case manager can help compare plans.
