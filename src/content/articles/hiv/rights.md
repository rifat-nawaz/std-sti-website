## Your protections

| Area | Protection |
|---|---|
| Jobs | Americans with Disabilities Act |
| Housing | Fair Housing Act |
| Health insurance | Can't be denied or charged more for HIV |
| Health records | Privacy laws |

## Working in health care

People with HIV work in health care, including many clinical roles. Specific rules for certain procedures follow professional guidance.

## Military

Policies on service and commissioning for people with HIV have changed in recent years. Check current Department of Defense rules.
