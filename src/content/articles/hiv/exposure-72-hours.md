## Hour by hour

| When | Do this |
|---|---|
| Now | Go to an ER or urgent care |
| Within 72 hours | Start PEP |
| 2 weeks | Test for chlamydia and gonorrhea |
| As your clinician schedules | HIV and syphilis tests |

## Situations that count

Condom broke, sex without a condom with a partner of unknown status, sharing needles, sexual assault, needle-stick injury.

See [how to get PEP fast](/hiv/where-to-get-pep/).
