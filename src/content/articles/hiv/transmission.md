## Fluids that can spread HIV

Blood, semen, pre-cum, rectal fluid, vaginal fluid, breast milk.

If you'd rather test privately, a [lab-processed home kit](/go/k1/) uses the same type of lab test as a clinic.

## Main routes

| Route | Risk |
|---|---|
| Receptive anal sex | Highest sexual risk |
| Insertive anal sex | Lower |
| Vaginal sex | Lower than anal |
| Sharing needles | High |
| Pregnancy, birth, breastfeeding | Preventable with care |
| Oral sex | Very low |

## Not a risk

Saliva, tears, sweat, kissing, hugging, sharing dishes, toilet seats, swimming pools, mosquitoes.

See [HIV risk by activity](/hiv/risk-per-act/).
