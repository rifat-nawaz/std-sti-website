## How we compare tests

We compare features you can check: test type, FDA status, lab certification, window period, support and privacy. We haven't lab-tested products ourselves.

## Options

| Option | Result | Conclusive after |
|---|---|---|
| Oral self-test | About 20 minutes at home | 90 days |
| Finger-prick mail-in kit (antigen/antibody) | A few days | 45 days in most labs' guidance |

## Free options

Many health departments and national programs mail free HIV self-tests. See [free HIV testing](/hiv/free-testing/).

## After a reactive result

Get a confirmatory lab test and contact a clinician. See [just diagnosed with HIV](/hiv/just-diagnosed/).
