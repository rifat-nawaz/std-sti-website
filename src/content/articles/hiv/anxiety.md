## Signs it may be anxiety

- Retesting after conclusive negatives
- Checking your body for symptoms many times a day
- Searching online for reassurance
- Worry that disrupts sleep or work

For privacy and convenience, an [at-home test kit](/go/k1/) processed by a certified lab is another option.

## What helps

| Step | Why |
|---|---|
| Trust a conclusive test | It's reliable |
| Limit symptom searching | It fuels anxiety |
| Talk to a clinician or therapist | Anxiety is treatable |
| Plan prevention for the future | PrEP and condoms reduce future worry |
