## Signs of advanced HIV

- Rapid, unexplained weight loss
- Fevers and night sweats that keep coming back
- Diarrhea lasting more than a week
- Sores in the mouth, anus or genitals
- Pneumonia or other serious infections
- Memory problems or depression

## Get emergency care for

Trouble breathing, confusion, severe headache or stiff neck.
