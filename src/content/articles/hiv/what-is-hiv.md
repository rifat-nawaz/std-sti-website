## How HIV affects the body

HIV enters CD4 cells, uses them to make copies of itself, and destroys them. Over years, falling CD4 counts leave the body open to infections it would normally fight off.

## Stages without care

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| Acute | Flu-like illness 2 to 4 weeks after infection, or no symptoms |
| Chronic | Often no symptoms for years |
| AIDS | Severe immune damage and serious infections |

## With care

Starting care early stops HIV from damaging the immune system. See [starting HIV care](/hiv/treatment/) and [U=U](/hiv/undetectable/).
