## What the numbers mean

| Test | Goal | Concern |
|---|---|---|
| Viral load | Undetectable (under 200) | Rising or repeatedly detectable |
| CD4 count | Above 500 | Below 200 (AIDS range) |

## How often they're checked

More often in the first months of care, then usually every 3 to 6 months once stable.

## Blips

A single low detectable result that goes back to undetectable is common and doesn't mean care has failed.
