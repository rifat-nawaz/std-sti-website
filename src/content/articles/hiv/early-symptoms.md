## Common acute symptoms

| Symptom | Notes |
|---|---|
| Fever | Often the first sign |
| Sore throat | Common |
| Rash | Flat or slightly raised, often on the trunk |
| Swollen glands | Neck, armpits, groin |
| Aches and tiredness | Like the flu |
| Mouth ulcers | Sometimes |

## What to do

Tell a clinician you may have been exposed and ask for a **lab antigen/antibody test** or an **RNA test**. See [HIV tests explained](/hiv/testing/).
