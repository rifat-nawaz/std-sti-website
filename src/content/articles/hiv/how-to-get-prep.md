## Where to get PrEP

| Option | Good to know |
|---|---|
| Primary care | Can also manage other health needs |
| Sexual health clinic | Experienced with PrEP |
| Telehealth | Convenient; labs arranged locally |
| Health department | Often low cost |

## Costs

Most plans cover PrEP and required tests without copays. Without insurance, ask about patient assistance and state programs.
