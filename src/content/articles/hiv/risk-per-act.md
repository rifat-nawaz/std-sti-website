## Estimated risk per 10,000 exposures

| Exposure | Risk per 10,000 |
|---|---|
| Receptive anal sex | 138 |
| Sharing needles | 63 |
| Insertive anal sex | 11 |
| Receptive vaginal sex | 8 |
| Insertive vaginal sex | 4 |
| Oral sex | Low |
| Biting, spitting, sharing sex toys | Negligible |

Source: CDC HIV Risk and Prevention Estimates.

## What lowers risk

| Method | Effect |
|---|---|
| Partner undetectable | No sexual transmission |
| PrEP used as prescribed | About 99% lower risk from sex |
| Condoms every time | Much lower risk |

Exposed in the last 72 hours? See [PEP](/hiv/pep/).
