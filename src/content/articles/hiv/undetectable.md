## What "undetectable" means

A viral load test can't measure HIV in the blood, or finds fewer than 200 copies per milliliter.

## What U=U covers

| Situation | Covered by U=U? |
|---|---|
| Vaginal, anal and oral sex | Yes |
| Sharing needles | Not established; don't share |
| Breastfeeding | Lower risk but not zero |
| Other STDs | No |

## How to know a partner is undetectable

Talk openly and ask about recent viral load results. Many couples share lab results.
