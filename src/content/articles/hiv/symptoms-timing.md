## Timing

| After exposure | What it means |
|---|---|
| 1 to 7 days | Symptoms unlikely to be HIV |
| 2 to 4 weeks | Typical time for acute symptoms |
| Months to years | Chronic HIV, often no symptoms |

Check test timing with the [window period calculator](/std-testing/window-period/).
