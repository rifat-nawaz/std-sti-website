## First steps

1. Confirm the result with a lab test, if not already done.
2. Get a referral to an HIV care provider.
3. Go to your first visit: blood tests and a care plan.
4. Ask for a case manager to help with costs.
5. Tell partners, yourself or through the health department.

## First visit tests

Viral load, CD4 count, resistance testing, hepatitis and other STD tests.

See [starting HIV care](/hiv/treatment/) and [paying for HIV care](/hiv/paying-for-care/).
