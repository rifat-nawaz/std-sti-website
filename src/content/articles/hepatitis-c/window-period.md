## Timing

| Test | Detects from |
|---|---|
| RNA | 1 to 2 weeks |
| Antibody | Usually 8 to 11 weeks |
| Reliable antibody negative | About 12 weeks |

See the [window period calculator](/std-testing/window-period/).
