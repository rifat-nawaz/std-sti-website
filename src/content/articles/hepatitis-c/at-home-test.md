## How we compare tests

We compare features you can check: test type, lab certification, follow-up and privacy. We haven't lab-tested products ourselves.

For privacy and convenience, an [at-home test kit](/go/k2/) processed by a certified lab is another option.

## Checklist

| Feature | Why |
|---|---|
| Antibody test | Screens for past or current infection |
| RNA follow-up | Confirms current infection |
| Clinician support | Links you to care |
