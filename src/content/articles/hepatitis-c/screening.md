## Who and how often

| Who | How often |
|---|---|
| All adults | At least once |
| Pregnant people | Every pregnancy |
| People who inject drugs | Regularly |
| People with HIV | At least yearly |
| After a blood exposure | As advised |

You can also order a [home test kit](/go/k1/), collect your sample yourself and mail it to a certified lab.
