## Sexual risk at a glance

| Situation | Risk |
|---|---|
| Long-term monogamous partner | Very low |
| Multiple partners | Higher |
| Anal sex, sex with blood | Higher |
| Gay and bisexual men with HIV | Higher; test regularly |
