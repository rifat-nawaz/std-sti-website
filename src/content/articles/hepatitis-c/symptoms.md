## Possible symptoms

| Early | Later (liver damage) |
|---|---|
| Tiredness | Swollen belly or legs |
| Nausea, poor appetite | Easy bruising |
| Dark urine | Confusion |
| Yellow skin or eyes | Vomiting blood |
