## What speeds damage

- Alcohol
- Fatty liver
- HIV or hepatitis B coinfection
- Older age at infection

You can also order a [home test kit](/go/k1/), collect your sample yourself and mail it to a certified lab.

## Get urgent care for

Yellow skin or eyes, swollen belly, confusion, vomiting blood.
