## Side by side

| | Acute | Chronic |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | First months | Ongoing |
| Symptoms | Often none | Usually none for years |
| Clears on its own? | Sometimes | Rarely |
| Curable with treatment? | Yes | Yes |
