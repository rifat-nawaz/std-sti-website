## How it spreads

| From | To | How |
|---|---|---|
| Mouth (cold sore) | Partner's genitals | Oral sex |
| Genitals | Partner's mouth | Oral sex |
| Your mouth | Your genitals | Uncommon once you already have HSV-1 |

## Protect partners

- No oral sex or kissing during a cold sore or tingling
- Dental dams or condoms for oral sex
