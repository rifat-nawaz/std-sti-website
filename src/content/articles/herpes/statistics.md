| Measure | Value |
|---|---|

| HSV-2, US ages 14 to 49 | 11.9% |
| HSV-2, worldwide ages 15 to 49 | About 520 million people |

## Why so many don't know

Most infections cause mild symptoms or none, and herpes isn't routinely tested. See [should you get tested for herpes?](/herpes/testing-without-symptoms/)
