## How the virus works

HSV enters through skin or mucous membranes, travels along nerves, and rests in a nerve cluster near the spine. From time to time it travels back to the skin, sometimes causing sores and sometimes shedding silently.

## How serious is it?

| For | Typical impact |
|---|---|
| Most adults | Occasional outbreaks, often mild |
| People with weakened immunity | More severe outbreaks |
| Newborns | Can be life-threatening |
