## Side by side

| | HSV-1 | HSV-2 |
|---|---|---|
| Usual location | Mouth (cold sores); increasingly genital | Genitals |
| Genital recurrences | Uncommon after the first year | More frequent, especially early on |
| Silent shedding (genital) | Low | Higher |
| US prevalence (ages 14 to 49) | 47.8% | 11.9% |
