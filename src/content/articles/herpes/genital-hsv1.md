## What to expect

| Feature | Genital HSV-1 |
|---|---|
| First outbreak | Can be as severe as HSV-2 |
| Later outbreaks | Uncommon |
| Silent shedding | Low, and falls over time |
| Passing to partners | Lower than HSV-2 |
