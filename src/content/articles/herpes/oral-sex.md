## Who can pass it to whom

| Situation | Risk |
|---|---|
| Giving oral sex with a cold sore | High risk to partner's genitals |
| Giving oral sex with no sore, but HSV-1 | Lower, still possible |
| Receiving oral from a partner with genital herpes | Possible oral infection |
