## Shedding by type

| Type and site | Silent shedding |
|---|---|
| Genital HSV-2 | More common, especially early |
| Genital HSV-1 | Less common |
| Oral HSV-1 | Common |

## Reducing the risk

- Daily medicine prescribed by a clinician
- Condoms or dental dams
- Avoiding sex during outbreaks and tingling
