## Stages

| Stage | Appearance |
|---|---|
| Blister | Tiny, clear or cloudy fluid bumps |
| Ulcer | Shallow, painful open sore with a red edge |
| Crust | Yellowish or brown scab |
| Healed | Normal or slightly discolored skin for a while |

## Atypical sores

Small splits or "paper cuts", itchy red patches, or a single small sore. See [herpes without blisters](/herpes/without-blisters/).
