## Tips

- Practice a short, factual disclosure.
- Choose a private setting.
- Share ways you lower risk: avoiding outbreaks, condoms, daily medicine.
- Lean on supportive friends or groups.

See [how to tell a partner you have herpes](/herpes/telling-a-partner/).
