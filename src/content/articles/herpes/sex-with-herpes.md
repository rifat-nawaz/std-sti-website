## Protecting your partner

| Step | Why |
|---|---|
| No sex during outbreaks or tingling | Highest risk times |
| Condoms or dental dams | Lower risk |
| Daily medicine | Reduces shedding and outbreaks |
| Open communication | Shared decisions |
