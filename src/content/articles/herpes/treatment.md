## What a clinician can do

| Step | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Swab a fresh sore | Confirms herpes and its type |
| Explain HSV-1 vs HSV-2 | Type affects how often outbreaks return |
| Prescribe medicine | Shortens outbreaks or prevents them |
| Plan for pregnancy | Lowers the risk to a newborn |
| Talk about partners | Ways to lower the chance of passing it on |

## Two ways to manage outbreaks

- **During outbreaks only:** start medicine at the first tingle or sore to shorten the outbreak.
- **Every day:** prevents most outbreaks and lowers the chance of passing herpes to a partner.

Your clinician will explain the options and which fits your outbreak pattern, relationship and plans.

## Questions to ask

- Is this HSV-1 or HSV-2?
- How often might outbreaks come back?
- Which approach do you recommend for me, and why?
- What should I do if I'm pregnant or planning a pregnancy?

## Don't

- Use someone else's medicine or leftover pills
- Buy unregulated "herpes cures" online
- Rely on over-the-counter cold sore products for genital outbreaks without advice

For comfort during an outbreak, see [herpes outbreak self-care](/herpes/outbreak-self-care/).
