## When to ask for confirmation

- HSV-2 index between 1.1 and 3.0
- Positive result with no symptoms ever
- Result doesn't fit your history

## Options

| Test | Notes |
|---|---|
| Herpes Western blot | Specialized lab; considered very accurate |
| Second type-specific assay | Different method from the first |
| PCR swab of a sore | Best if you get symptoms |
