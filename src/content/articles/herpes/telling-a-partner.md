## Example scripts

> "Before things go further, I want to tell you I have genital herpes. It's really common, I know my triggers, and there are ways we can lower the risk. I'm happy to answer any questions."

> "I have HSV-1, the same virus as cold sores, on my genitals. It rarely comes back, but I wanted you to know so we can decide together."

## Handling reactions

Some partners are relieved by the facts; others need time. Their reaction isn't a judgment of your worth.
