## What lowers risk

| Step | Effect |
|---|---|
| No sex during outbreaks or tingling | Avoids highest-risk times |
| Condoms every time | Lowers risk |
| Daily medicine for partner with herpes | About half the risk in a major study |
| All three together | Lowest risk |

## Who is more at risk

Women with male partners who have herpes, and people without HSV-1 antibodies.
