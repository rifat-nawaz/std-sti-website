## Triggers and tips

| Trigger | Tip |
|---|---|
| Stress | Sleep, exercise, support |
| Illness, fever | Rest |
| Friction | Lubricant, loose clothing |
| Periods | Note timing in a diary |
| Sun (cold sores) | Lip sunscreen |
