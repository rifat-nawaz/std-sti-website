## Risk at a glance

| Activity | Risk |
|---|---|
| Kissing during a cold sore | High (HSV-1) |
| Kissing with no sore | Low |
| Sharing drinks or utensils during a cold sore | Low |
| Towels, toilet seats, pools | Negligible |
