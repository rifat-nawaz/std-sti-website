## Common triggers

| Trigger | Tips |
|---|---|
| Stress | Sleep, exercise and support can help |
| Illness or fever | Rest; outbreaks may follow a cold or flu |
| Fatigue | Regular sleep |
| Friction | Lubricant during sex, loose clothing |
| Periods | Note the timing in a diary |
| Weakened immune system | Talk to a clinician |

## Keep an outbreak diary

Note the date, how long it lasted, how bad it was, and anything unusual beforehand. Bring it to your clinician.

## When to see a clinician

- Outbreaks are frequent, long or very painful
- They affect your relationships, work or mood
- Sores take more than 2 weeks to heal
- You're pregnant or planning a pregnancy

Your clinician can discuss daily medicine to prevent outbreaks and check for anything that may be weakening your immune response. See [getting care for genital herpes](/herpes/treatment/).
