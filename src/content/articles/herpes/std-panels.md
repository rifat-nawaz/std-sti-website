answer: "Usually <strong>not</strong>. Most standard STD panels test for chlamydia, gonorrhea, HIV and syphilis but <strong>leave out herpes</strong>, because the CDC doesn't recommend routine herpes blood tests without symptoms. If you have a <strong>sore</strong>, ask for a <strong>swab</strong>; otherwise, discuss whether a blood test makes sense."

## How to ask

- With a sore: "Can you swab this for herpes PCR?"
- Without symptoms: "Should I have a type-specific herpes IgG test, given my situation?"
