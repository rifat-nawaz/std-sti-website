## First steps

1. Confirm HSV-1 or HSV-2 with a swab or blood test.
2. See a clinician about [getting care](/herpes/treatment/).
3. Learn your triggers.
4. Plan disclosure: see [telling a partner](/herpes/telling-a-partner/).
5. Find support: see [coping with a herpes diagnosis](/herpes/coping/).
