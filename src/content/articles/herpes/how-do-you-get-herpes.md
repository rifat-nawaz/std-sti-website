## Routes

| Route | Risk |
|---|---|
| Sex during an outbreak | Highest |
| Sex without symptoms (silent shedding) | Lower, but common way it spreads |
| Oral sex with a cold sore | High for genital HSV-1 |
| Kissing with a cold sore | High for oral HSV-1 |
| Birth | Risk to newborns |
