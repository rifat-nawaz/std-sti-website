## What helps

| Approach | Why |
|---|---|
| Accurate information | Replaces fear with facts |
| Support groups | Connection with people who understand |
| Counseling | Helps with shame and anxiety |
| Time | Most people feel better over months |

## Crisis support

If you're thinking about harming yourself, call or text **988** any time.
