## Reading your index value (typical lab cutoffs)

| Index | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Under 0.9 | Negative |
| 0.9 to 1.1 | Equivocal; retest |
| 1.1 to 3.0 (HSV-2) | Low positive; confirm |
| Above 3.0 (HSV-2) | More likely a true positive |
