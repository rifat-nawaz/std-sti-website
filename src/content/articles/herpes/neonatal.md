## Warning signs in a newborn

- Blisters on the skin, eyes or mouth
- Fever or low temperature
- Poor feeding
- Unusual sleepiness or irritability
- Seizures

**Call 911 or go to the emergency room.**

## Prevention

- Tell your provider if you or your partner have herpes.
- No kissing babies with cold sores.
- Wash hands often around newborns.
