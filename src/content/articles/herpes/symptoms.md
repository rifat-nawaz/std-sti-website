## Signs of an outbreak

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| Prodrome | Tingling, itching, nerve pain |
| Blisters | Small fluid-filled bumps, often in clusters |
| Sores | Blisters break into shallow, painful sores |
| Crusting | Sores scab and heal |
