## Risk to the baby

| Situation | Risk |
|---|---|
| Herpes from before pregnancy | Low |
| First infection in early pregnancy | Lower |
| First infection near delivery | Highest |

## Your care plan

Your provider may suggest medicine from late pregnancy to prevent an outbreak at delivery, and a C-section if sores or tingling are present when labor starts.

See [neonatal herpes](/herpes/neonatal/).
