## Compare

| Condition | Clues |
|---|---|
| Herpes | Painful clustered blisters, tingling first, may recur |
| Ingrown hair | Single bump with a hair, after shaving |
| Pimple or folliculitis | White head, around hair follicles |
| Razor burn | Red rash after shaving |
| Friction sore | After rough sex, heals in days |
| Yeast or jock itch | Itchy red rash, no blisters |
| Syphilis | Firm, painless sore |
