## Where sores appear

- Labia and vulva
- Inside the vagina or on the cervix
- Buttocks, thighs, around the anus

## Other signs

Painful urination, discharge, swollen glands, flu-like symptoms during a first outbreak.

See [herpes and pregnancy](/herpes/pregnancy/).
