## Possible complications

| Complication | Warning signs |
|---|---|
| Meningitis | Severe headache, stiff neck, light sensitivity |
| Urinary retention | Can't pee |
| Nerve pain | Burning or aching in buttocks or legs |
| Eye infection | Pain, redness, blurred vision |
| Rectal inflammation | Rectal pain, discharge |

See [eye herpes](/herpes/eye/).
