answer: "Herpes <strong>IgM tests aren't recommended</strong>. They produce <strong>frequent false positives</strong>, can't reliably tell HSV-1 from HSV-2, and <strong>can't show whether an infection is new</strong>, since IgM can appear in recurrences too. Ask for a <strong>PCR swab</strong> of a sore or a <strong>type-specific IgG</strong> test."
takeaways:
  - "A positive IgM alone shouldn't be used to diagnose herpes."
  - "Some home and online labs still offer IgM; skip it."
  - "If you were told you have herpes based on IgM, ask about retesting."
  - "Talk to a clinician about the right test."
