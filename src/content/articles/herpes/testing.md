## Which test?

| Situation | Best test |
|---|---|
| Fresh sore | PCR swab |
| No symptoms, partner has herpes | IgG blood test, discussed with a clinician |
| Low-positive HSV-2 result | Confirmatory test |
| Pregnancy planning | Discuss with your provider |
