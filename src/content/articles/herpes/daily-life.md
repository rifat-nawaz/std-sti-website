## Tips

| Activity | During an outbreak |
|---|---|
| Exercise | Loose, breathable clothes; shower after |
| Swimming, hot tubs | Fine for others; may sting sores |
| Shaving, waxing | Avoid the affected area |
| Periods | Use what's comfortable; change often |
| Sharing towels | Avoid during an outbreak |
