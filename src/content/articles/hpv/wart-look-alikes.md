## Compare

| Bump | Clues |
|---|---|
| Genital wart | Soft, irregular, can cluster, may grow |
| Pearly penile papules | Even rows around the head of the penis; don't change |
| Fordyce spots | Tiny yellow-white dots on penis or labia |
| Vestibular papillomatosis | Symmetrical finger-like bumps inside the vulva |
| Skin tag | Soft flap on a stalk |
| Molluscum | Firm, dome-shaped with a central dimple |
| Ingrown hair | Red bump with a hair |
