## From HPV to cancer

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| HPV infection | Most clear in 2 years |
| Persistent infection | Years |
| Precancer (CIN 2 or 3) | Can be found and removed |
| Cancer | Usually 10 to 20 years after infection |

## Prevention

[HPV vaccine](/hpv/vaccine/) and [regular screening](/hpv/hpv-test/).
