## Screening by age

| Age | Options |
|---|---|
| Under 21 | No screening |
| 21 to 29 | Pap every 3 years |
| 30 to 65 | HPV test every 5 years, co-test every 5 years, or Pap every 3 years |
| Over 65 | Stop if past results were normal |

## Self-collection

Self-collected HPV samples work well. In 2025, the FDA approved the first device for collecting your own sample at home. See [at-home HPV tests](/hpv/at-home-test/).
