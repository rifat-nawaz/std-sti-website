## Signs the skin has healed

- No open sores, scabs or raw patches
- No pain when touched
- No oozing or bleeding

## After healing

- Use condoms or dental dams with partners.
- Check for new warts and get them looked at early.
- Avoid shaving the area for a while to prevent spreading warts.

## When to call your clinician

- Pain, swelling or pus at the treated area
- Bleeding that doesn't stop
- Healing that takes longer than expected
