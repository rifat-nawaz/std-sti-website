## Where they appear

- Shaft or head of the penis
- Scrotum and groin
- Upper thighs
- In or around the anus

## Getting checked

A primary care clinician, sexual health clinic, dermatologist or urologist can confirm warts and discuss removal. See [getting care for HPV](/hpv/treatment/).
