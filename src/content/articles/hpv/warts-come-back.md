## Why warts return

- Removal treats the wart you can see, not every infected cell nearby.
- Tiny warts that weren't visible can grow later.
- Smoking, stress and a weakened immune system make returns more likely.

## What helps

- **Don't smoke.**
- **Avoid shaving or waxing** the area while warts are active.
- **Go back early** if new warts appear; small warts are easier to remove.
- **Get vaccinated** if you're eligible, to protect against HPV types you haven't had.

## When to get checked

- Warts that bleed, change color or grow quickly
- Warts inside the anus or vagina
- Warts that keep coming back after many treatments
