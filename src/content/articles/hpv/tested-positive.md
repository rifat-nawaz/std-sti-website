## Checklist

1. Read your result: see [HPV test results](/hpv/test-results/).
2. Book any follow-up your clinician advises.
3. Stop smoking if you smoke.
4. Consider the [vaccine](/hpv/vaccine-after-infection/).
5. Talk to partners if you choose.
