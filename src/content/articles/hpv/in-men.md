## How men find out

- Genital warts appear
- A partner tests positive
- Rarely, through an anal Pap test or a cancer diagnosis

## What to do

Nothing, if you have no symptoms. See a clinician for warts, and consider the [HPV vaccine](/hpv/vaccine/) if eligible.
