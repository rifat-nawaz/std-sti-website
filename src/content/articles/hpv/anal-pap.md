## Who may need screening

- People with HIV, especially gay and bisexual men and transgender women
- Some people with organ transplants or weakened immunity
- People with a history of vulvar or cervical precancer, in some cases

## What happens

A swab is gently inserted into the anus. If results are abnormal, a specialist examines the area with magnification.
