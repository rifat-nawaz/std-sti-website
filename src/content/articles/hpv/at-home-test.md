## How we compare tests

We compare features you can check: FDA status, who the test is for, lab processing and follow-up support. We haven't lab-tested products ourselves.

## Who it's for

| Suitable | Not suitable |
|---|---|
| Ages 25 to 65, average risk | History of cervical precancer or cancer |
| Due for routine screening | Recent abnormal results |
| | Weakened immune system |

## After a positive result

You'll be referred for a Pap test or colposcopy. See [HPV test results](/hpv/test-results/).
