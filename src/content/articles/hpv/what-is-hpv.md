## HPV, warts and cancer

| | Low-risk HPV | High-risk HPV |
|---|---|---|
| Examples | Types 6 and 11 | Types 16 and 18, and others |
| Can cause | Genital warts | Precancer and cancer |
| Usually | Clears or causes warts | Clears; sometimes persists |
