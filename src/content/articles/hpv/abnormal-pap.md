## Pap terms

| Result | Meaning | Usual next step |
|---|---|---|
| ASC-US | Unclear mild changes | HPV test or repeat |
| LSIL | Low-grade changes | Repeat or colposcopy |
| ASC-H | Can't rule out high-grade | Colposcopy |
| HSIL | High-grade changes | Colposcopy, often treatment |
| AGC | Atypical glandular cells | Colposcopy and further tests |
