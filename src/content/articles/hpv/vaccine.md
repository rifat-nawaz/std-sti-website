## Who should get it

| Age | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| 9 to 12 | Recommended, ideally at 11 or 12 |
| 13 to 26 | Recommended if not fully vaccinated |
| 27 to 45 | Decide with a clinician |
| Over 45 | Not currently recommended |

## Why age matters

The vaccine prevents infection; it doesn't clear HPV you already have. That's why it works best before someone becomes sexually active.

## Where to get it

Doctors' offices, pharmacies and health departments. Most insurance covers it for recommended ages, and the Vaccines for Children program covers eligible children.

## After vaccination

- Keep up with cervical screening from age 21.
- Keep using condoms with new partners to lower the risk of other STDs.
