## Signs to get checked

- Mouth or tongue warts
- Sore throat lasting more than 3 weeks
- A lump in the neck
- Pain when swallowing, or ear pain on one side
- Hoarseness

See [HPV and throat cancer](/hpv/throat-cancer/).
