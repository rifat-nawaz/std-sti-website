## Who is at higher risk

| Group | Main concern |
|---|---|
| Men with HIV | Anal cancer |
| Gay and bisexual men | Anal cancer |
| Smokers | All HPV cancers |
| Uncircumcised men with poor hygiene | Penile cancer (rare) |
