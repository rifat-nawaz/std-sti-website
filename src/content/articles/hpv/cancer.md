## Cancers linked to HPV

| Cancer | Notes |
|---|---|
| Cervical | Nearly all caused by HPV |
| Oropharyngeal (throat) | About 70% linked to HPV |
| Anal | Most caused by HPV |
| Penile, vaginal, vulvar | Many caused by HPV |

## Prevention

[HPV vaccine](/hpv/vaccine/) · [Cervical screening](/hpv/hpv-test/) · Not smoking
