## Why it matters

Precancer develops slowly, usually over many years of persistent high-risk HPV. Follow-up catches changes long before cancer.

## Positive, negative, then positive?

HPV can drop below detectable levels and reappear, or a new infection may occur. Your clinician considers your full history.

See [colposcopy](/hpv/colposcopy/).
