## Giving vs receiving

Giving oral sex is the main way HPV reaches the mouth. Receiving oral sex from someone with oral HPV can pass it to the genitals.

See [oral HPV symptoms](/hpv/oral/) and [HPV and throat cancer](/hpv/throat-cancer/).
