## Testing options for men

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Visual exam | Diagnoses warts |
| Anal Pap test | Some higher-risk men, especially with HIV |
| Blood test for HPV | Not used for diagnosis |
| Oral HPV test | Research only |
