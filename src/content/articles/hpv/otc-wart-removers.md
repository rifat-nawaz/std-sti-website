## Why they're risky

| Product | Risk on genital skin |
|---|---|
| Wart removers for hands and feet | Chemical burns, painful sores |
| Home freezing kits | Frostbite-type injury, scarring |
| Apple cider vinegar | Acid burns |
| Tea tree oil, garlic | Irritation, allergic reactions |
| Strong acids bought online | Deep burns |

## What to do instead

1. **Get it checked.** A clinician can confirm whether it's a wart.
2. **Discuss options.** Warts can be left to clear, removed in the office, or treated with prescription products used at home.
3. **Don't shave or pick** the area, which can spread warts.
