## Genital warts

- **Diagnosis:** a clinician looks at the warts. No blood test is needed.
- **Options:** warts may go away on their own within months. A clinician can also remove them in the office or prescribe treatment you use at home.
- **Coming back:** warts can return, especially in the first few months. That doesn't mean you were reinfected.

Don't use over-the-counter wart removers meant for hands or feet. They can burn genital skin.

## Abnormal Pap or positive HPV test

| Next step | What it is |
|---|---|
| Repeat test | Many changes clear on their own, so you may simply test again in a year |
| Colposcopy | A closer look at the cervix with a magnifier, sometimes with a small biopsy |
| Removal of abnormal cells | A short procedure, such as LEEP, if changes are more advanced |

Follow-up is based on your results and history. Keep every follow-up appointment.

## Things that help

- **Don't smoke.** Smoking makes it harder for the body to clear HPV.
- **Keep up with screening.**
- **Get vaccinated** if you're eligible. See the [HPV vaccine](/hpv/vaccine/).
