## What the evidence supports

| Step | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Stop smoking | Smoking weakens the local immune response and is linked to persistent HPV |
| Keep up with screening | Catches cell changes early, when they're easy to manage |
| Get the HPV vaccine if eligible | Protects against types you haven't had |
| Use condoms | Lowers the chance of new infections |

## What isn't proven

- Vitamins and herbal supplements sold for HPV
- "Detox" products and immune boosters
- Creams or vaginal products marketed to clear HPV

These can be costly, and some may interact with medicines. Ask a clinician before trying any.

## Waiting it out

It's frustrating to wait, but for most people HPV clears without any treatment. Your screening schedule is designed to watch it safely.
