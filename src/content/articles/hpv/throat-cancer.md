## Warning signs to check

- Lump in the neck
- Sore throat or ear pain on one side lasting 3 weeks or more
- Trouble or pain swallowing
- Hoarseness

## Lowering your risk

HPV vaccine, not smoking, limiting alcohol, and fewer oral sex partners.
