## Common results and next steps

| HPV result | Pap result | Usual next step |
|---|---|---|
| Negative | Normal | Routine screening |
| Other high-risk positive | Normal | Repeat in 1 year |
| HPV 16 or 18 positive | Any | Colposcopy |
| Positive | Abnormal | Colposcopy, often |

See [abnormal Pap results](/hpv/abnormal-pap/) and [colposcopy](/hpv/colposcopy/).
