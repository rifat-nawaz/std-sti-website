## Step 1: Get a diagnosis

Chlamydia is confirmed with a NAAT on urine or a swab. If you tested at home, the result is valid for treatment. Many kit companies include a clinician consult.

You may be treated **before your results come back** if you have symptoms or a partner has tested positive.

## Step 2: Get seen by a clinician

| Where | Good to know |
|---|---|
| Health department STD clinic | Often free or low cost; may treat the same day |
| Your own doctor | Can also check for other STDs |
| Urgent care | Open evenings and weekends |
| Telehealth | Fast if you already have a positive result |
| Planned Parenthood or community clinic | Sliding-scale fees |

See [free and low-cost testing](/std-testing/free-testing/) to find a clinic.

## Step 3: What happens at the visit

- The clinician asks about symptoms, the kind of sex you've had and when, and any pregnancy.
- They may test other body sites (throat, rectum) and test for gonorrhea, HIV and syphilis.
- They prescribe treatment and explain exactly how to take it.

Ask any question you have. There's no wrong question, and clinicians hear these every day.

## Step 4: While you're being treated

- **Follow the instructions** and finish everything prescribed, even if you feel better.
- **Avoid all sex**, including oral and anal sex, until you finish treatment and your clinician says it's safe. See [how long to wait before sex](/chlamydia/sex-after-treatment/).
- **Don't share or use leftover medicine.**

## Step 5: Tell partners

Partners from the **last 60 days** should be tested and treated, or your most recent partner if it's been longer. If they aren't treated, you can be reinfected.

- Tell them yourself. Our guide on [telling a partner](/guides/telling-a-partner/) has sample messages.
- Ask the health department to notify them anonymously.
- Ask your clinician about **expedited partner therapy**: in most states, they can give you treatment to pass on.

## Step 6: Retest

Retest **about 3 months after treatment**. If you're pregnant, you'll also have a test about 4 weeks after treatment. Read [when and why to retest](/chlamydia/retesting/).

## When to go back sooner

- Symptoms that don't improve after you finish treatment
- New pelvic, belly or testicle pain
- A partner wasn't treated and you had sex again
