## Types of quick tests

| Test | Accuracy | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FDA-authorized home molecular test | High | Results at home; currently for people with a vagina |
| Clinic point-of-care molecular test | High | Results during or soon after the visit |
| Rapid antigen strip test sold online | Lower | Can miss infections; faint lines are confusing |
| Lab NAAT (home kit or clinic) | High | Results in a few days |

## If you use a strip test

A negative result doesn't rule out chlamydia. Confirm any result with a lab NAAT, especially if you have symptoms or a partner tested positive.

## Positive at home?

See a clinician for treatment. See [getting treated for chlamydia](/chlamydia/treatment/).
