## 15 myths and the facts

| Myth | Fact |
|---|---|
| You'd know if you had it | Most people have no symptoms |
| You can catch it from a toilet seat | It spreads through sex, not surfaces |
| Only people with many partners get it | One partner with chlamydia is enough |
| Condoms make you 100% safe | They lower the risk a lot, but not completely |
| It goes away by itself | Some infections clear, many don't, and damage can happen meanwhile |
| Once cured, you're immune | You can get it again |
| A Pap smear tests for it | Pap tests check cervical cells, not chlamydia |
| Oral sex is risk-free | Chlamydia can infect the throat |
| Men can't get it | Men get it and spread it, often without symptoms |
| A urine test covers everything | It misses throat and rectal infections |
| It's a virus | It's bacteria, which is why it's curable |
| Home remedies work | Only clinician-prescribed treatment cures it |
| It's only a problem for women | Men can get epididymitis and reactive arthritis |
| A positive test proves cheating | Chlamydia can go unnoticed for months or years |
| You only need to test if you have symptoms | Screening is recommended without symptoms for many people |

## The bottom line

If you're sexually active, especially under 25 or with a new partner, regular testing is the only reliable way to know. See [how to test for chlamydia](/chlamydia/testing/).
