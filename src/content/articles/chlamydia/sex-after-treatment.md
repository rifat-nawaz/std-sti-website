## The rule

CDC guidance is to avoid sex until all three are true:

1. **7 days have passed** since you finished treatment
2. **Your symptoms are gone**
3. **Every partner has been treated** and has also waited

## Why it matters

- The infection can still spread while treatment is working.
- An untreated partner can give chlamydia straight back to you. This "ping-pong" reinfection is one of the most common reasons chlamydia returns.

## Partners on different timelines

If you were treated on Monday and your partner on Friday, count from your partner's finish date. Wait until you have **both** waited the full time.

## After the wait

- Use condoms with new or untested partners.
- Retest about 3 months after treatment. See [chlamydia came back?](/chlamydia/reinfection/)
