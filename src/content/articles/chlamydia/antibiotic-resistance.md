## Chlamydia vs gonorrhea

| | Chlamydia | Gonorrhea |
|---|---|---|
| Resistance a major problem? | No | Yes |
| Main reason infection returns | Reinfection | Reinfection, and sometimes resistance |
| Follow-up | Retest at 3 months | Retest at 3 months; test of cure for throat infections |

## What looks like resistance

- Sex before you and your partner finished treatment
- A partner who was never treated
- Treatment not finished
- Testing too soon after treatment, picking up dead bacteria

## How to help keep treatment working

- Use antibiotics only when prescribed, for the condition they're prescribed for.
- Finish every course.
- Make sure partners are treated.
