## Common signs

| Area | Symptoms |
|---|---|
| Joints | Pain and swelling, often knees, ankles, feet |
| Tendons | Heel pain, swollen fingers or toes |
| Eyes | Redness, pain, light sensitivity |
| Urinary tract | Burning, discharge |
| Skin | Rash on palms or soles (less common) |

## What to do

- Get tested for chlamydia and other STDs.
- See a clinician; you may be referred to a rheumatologist.
- Get urgent care for eye pain or vision changes.

## Recovery

Most people improve within 3 to 12 months. A few have flares or long-term joint problems.
