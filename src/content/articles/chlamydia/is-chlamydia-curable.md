## What "cured" means

After treatment, the bacteria are gone and you can't pass chlamydia on, once you've waited the recommended time. Most people don't need a test to prove it, except during pregnancy or if symptoms continue.

## What treatment can't undo

| Possible lasting effect | Why |
|---|---|
| Scarred fallopian tubes | Caused by pelvic inflammatory disease before treatment |
| Higher risk of ectopic pregnancy | Linked to tubal scarring |
| Long-term pelvic pain | Can follow PID |

Most people who are treated early have no lasting effects.

## Is chlamydia permanent?

No. It doesn't stay in your body after a cure. If you test positive again later, it's a new infection.
