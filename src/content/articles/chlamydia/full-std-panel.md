## What a sensible panel includes

| Test | Why |
|---|---|
| Chlamydia and gonorrhea | Often occur together; same sample |
| HIV | Recommended for everyone 15 to 65 and after new STDs |
| Syphilis | Rising in the US; often silent |
| Trichomoniasis | Common; often missing from panels |
| Hepatitis C | Recommended once for every adult |
| Hepatitis B | Recommended once for every adult |

## What usually isn't needed

Herpes blood tests without symptoms are not recommended, because results can be misleading.
