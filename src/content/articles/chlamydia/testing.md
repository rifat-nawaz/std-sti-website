## Test types

| Sample | Best for |
|---|---|
| Vaginal swab | People with a vagina; most accurate, often self-collected |
| First-catch urine | People with a penis; also an option for others |
| Rectal swab | After receptive anal sex |
| Throat swab | After oral sex, if advised |

## 3-site testing

If you've had oral, anal and genital sex, test all three sites. Each sample is tested separately. See [3-site STD testing](/std-testing/three-site-testing/).

## Where to test

- Your doctor or a sexual health clinic
- Health department (often free)
- Lab-processed home kit

## Do I need to ask?

Yes. A routine urinalysis, Pap test or blood test doesn't check for chlamydia. Say: "Please test me for chlamydia and gonorrhea."
