## Possible causes of genital itching

| Cause | Clues |
|---|---|
| Chlamydia | Mild itch, with burning or discharge |
| Yeast infection | Strong itch, thick white discharge |
| Trichomoniasis | Itch, frothy discharge, fishy smell |
| Herpes | Tingling or itching before blisters appear |
| Irritation | After new soaps, shaving or tight clothing |
| Pubic lice | Itching in pubic hair, tiny lice or eggs |

## What to do

- Avoid scented products and tight clothing.
- Get tested if itching comes with discharge, burning or sores, or after a new partner.
- Don't use yeast products repeatedly without a diagnosis.
