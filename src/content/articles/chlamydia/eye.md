## Symptoms

- Red, irritated eye
- Watery or sticky discharge
- Swollen eyelid
- Feeling of grit in the eye
- Symptoms that last weeks

## How it gets into the eye

Infected semen or vaginal fluid reaching the eye, usually by touching the eye with unwashed hands after sex.

## Diagnosis

An eye swab can detect chlamydia. Because most people with eye infection also have a genital infection, a urine or genital swab test is recommended too.

## Get urgent care if

- Vision becomes blurry
- The eye is very painful or sensitive to light

Treatment is decided by a clinician. Partners should be tested.
