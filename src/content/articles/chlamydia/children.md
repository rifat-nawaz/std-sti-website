## Newborns vs older children

| Age | Most likely source |
|---|---|
| Newborn to a few months | Birth |
| Up to about 2 to 3 years | Possibly birth; needs careful assessment |
| Older children | Sexual contact or abuse |

## What to do

1. Take the child to a pediatrician or children's hospital.
2. Don't question the child in detail yourself; let trained professionals do it.
3. If abuse is suspected, contact child protective services or the police.
4. In an emergency, call 911.

## Support

The Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline is 1-800-422-4453.
