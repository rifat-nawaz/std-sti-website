## Cost by option

| Option | Typical cost |
|---|---|
| Health department STD clinic | Free or low cost |
| Planned Parenthood or community health center | Sliding scale by income |
| Your doctor, with insurance | Often $0 for screening; copay if for symptoms |
| Urgent care | Visit fee plus lab costs |
| At-home kit | Paid upfront; varies by provider and panel |

## Ways to save

- Use [free testing](/std-testing/free-testing/) through a health department.
- Ask if a clinic bundles chlamydia and gonorrhea in one test.
- Use HSA or FSA funds for home kits.
- Compare kit prices and what's included, such as a clinician consult.
