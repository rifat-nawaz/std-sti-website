## How we compare kits

We compare kits on things you can check: test type, sample type, lab certification, FDA status, clinician support and privacy. We haven't lab-tested kits ourselves. Confirm current details on the provider's site.

## Checklist before you buy

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| NAAT test | The most accurate test for chlamydia |
| CLIA-certified lab | Required for reliable US lab testing |
| Vaginal swab option | More accurate than urine for people with a vagina |
| Throat and rectal swabs | Needed after oral or anal sex |
| Gonorrhea included | Same sample, same test; the two often occur together |
| Clinician consult | So a positive result leads straight to treatment |
| Clear privacy policy | Health data is sensitive |

## Kit types

- **Single chlamydia and gonorrhea kit:** the best value if that's your only concern.
- **Multi-test panel:** adds HIV, syphilis and trichomoniasis. Good after a new partner.
- **Results-at-home test:** fastest, currently for people with a vagina.

## Timing

Test about **2 weeks after** a possible exposure. See the [window period calculator](/std-testing/window-period/).
