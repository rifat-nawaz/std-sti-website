## LGV vs regular chlamydia

| | Regular chlamydia | LGV |
|---|---|---|
| Spread | Genital surface | Into lymph tissue |
| Symptoms | Often none | Sores, swollen glands, rectal pain and bleeding |
| Care | Standard course | Longer course, chosen by a clinician |

## Stages

1. **Small painless sore** that heals quickly, often unnoticed
2. **Swollen, painful lymph nodes** in the groin, or **proctitis**: rectal pain, discharge, bleeding, constipation
3. **Rarely,** if untreated: scarring and strictures

## Testing

A rectal or genital chlamydia NAAT detects the infection. Tell your clinician about rectal symptoms; they may treat for LGV even if specific LGV testing isn't available.
