## Symptoms by body site

| Site | Possible symptoms |
|---|---|
| Vagina or cervix | Unusual discharge, burning when peeing, bleeding between periods or after sex, pain during sex |
| Penis | Clear or cloudy discharge, burning or itching at the tip, pain or swelling in one testicle |
| Rectum | Pain, discharge, bleeding |
| Throat | Usually none; sometimes a sore throat |
| Eye | Redness, discharge, irritation |

## First signs

Early symptoms are often mild: a little more discharge than usual, or a slight sting when you pee. Many people mistake them for a urinary infection or irritation.

## Do symptoms come and go?

Yes. Symptoms can fade even though the infection is still there. Fading symptoms don't mean you're cured.

## Get care today if you have

- Severe lower belly or pelvic pain, especially with fever
- Sudden, severe testicle pain
- Pain with a missed period or positive pregnancy test
