## Who to tell

Anyone you've had vaginal, anal or oral sex with in the last 60 days. If you haven't had sex in 60 days, tell your most recent partner.

## Message examples

**In person:**
> "I tested positive for chlamydia. It's really common and easy to cure, but you should get tested and treated too, even if you feel fine."

**Text:**
> "Hi, I just found out I have chlamydia. You might have it without symptoms. A clinic or health department can test and treat you this week."

**To an ex:**
> "This is a bit awkward, but I tested positive for chlamydia and you should get checked. Happy to send info on free testing."

## Anonymous options

Health department partner services can tell partners without naming you. Your clinician may also offer expedited partner therapy. See [do both partners need treatment?](/chlamydia/partner-treatment/)
