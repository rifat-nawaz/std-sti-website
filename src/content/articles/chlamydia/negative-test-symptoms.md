## Possible causes

| Cause | Clues |
|---|---|
| Tested too early | Exposure less than 2 weeks before testing |
| Wrong site | Oral or anal sex, but only urine tested |
| Gonorrhea | Thick discharge; usually tested together |
| Trichomoniasis | Itching, frothy discharge, smell |
| *Mycoplasma genitalium* | Burning or discharge with negative tests |
| BV or yeast | Discharge changes, smell or itch |
| Urinary infection | Frequent, urgent peeing |
| Irritation | New soaps, products or friction |

For privacy and convenience, an [at-home test kit](/go/k1/) processed by a certified lab is another option.

## What to ask the clinician

"My chlamydia test was negative, but I still have symptoms. Can you test me for gonorrhea, trichomoniasis and *Mycoplasma genitalium*, and check for BV, yeast and a urine infection?"

See [Mycoplasma genitalium](/mycoplasma-genitalium/).
