## Possible complications

| Complication | Signs |
|---|---|
| Epididymitis | Pain, swelling, warmth beside one testicle |
| Proctitis | Rectal pain, discharge, bleeding |
| Reactive arthritis | Joint pain, eye redness, weeks after infection |
| Urethral stricture | Rare; weak stream after repeated inflammation |

## Fertility, prostate and erections

- **Fertility:** epididymitis can occasionally affect sperm, but evidence of lasting infertility from chlamydia alone is limited.
- **Prostate:** a link to chronic prostatitis is debated.
- **Erections:** chlamydia isn't a known cause.

## Get care if you have

Testicle pain, blood in semen or rectal symptoms. Sudden severe testicle pain needs an emergency room.
