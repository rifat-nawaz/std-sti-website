## Myths that don't work

| Remedy | The facts |
|---|---|
| Garlic, echinacea, goldenseal | No evidence they cure chlamydia |
| Apple cider vinegar or douching | Can irritate tissue and push infection higher |
| Cranberry juice | May help some urinary infections, not chlamydia |
| Tea tree oil | Can burn and irritate genital skin |
| Waiting it out | Some infections clear, but many don't, and damage can happen meanwhile |

## Why self-treatment with antibiotics fails

- **Wrong medicine:** many antibiotics don't work against chlamydia.
- **Wrong amount or length:** partial treatment may not clear the infection.
- **Unsafe sources:** medicine sold online without a prescription, or for animals, may be fake, expired or contaminated.
- **Missed partners and tests:** self-treatment skips partner treatment, other STD tests and the 3-month retest.

## Faster ways to real treatment

- Health department clinics, often free and same-day
- Telehealth after a home test
- Urgent care
