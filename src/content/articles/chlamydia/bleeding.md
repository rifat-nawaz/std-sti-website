## How chlamydia causes bleeding

Chlamydia can cause **cervicitis**: inflammation that makes the cervix fragile, so it bleeds easily during sex or between periods.

## Can it change your period?

- **Late period:** not a typical effect. Take a pregnancy test.
- **Heavier or more painful periods:** possible if the infection spreads to the uterus (PID).

## Other causes of bleeding after sex

- Gonorrhea or trichomoniasis
- Cervical polyps or ectropion
- Hormonal contraception
- Less often, cervical cell changes; keep up with screening

## When to get care today

- Heavy bleeding
- Bleeding with severe pelvic pain or fever
- Bleeding with a positive pregnancy test
