## Possible explanations

- **No transmission yet:** not every exposure passes chlamydia on.
- **Too early:** the test was within about 2 weeks of the last exposure.
- **Wrong site:** a throat or rectal infection wasn't tested.
- **Different source:** one partner was infected elsewhere.
- **Rare test error.**

## What to do

1. See a clinician. You'll likely be treated anyway.
2. Retest any untested sites.
3. Avoid sex until both of you have finished treatment.
