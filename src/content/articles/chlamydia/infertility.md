## How chlamydia affects fertility

Inflammation from PID can scar or block the fallopian tubes, stopping the egg and sperm from meeting, or trapping a fertilized egg in the tube (ectopic pregnancy).

You can also order a [home test kit](/go/k1/), collect your sample yourself and mail it to a certified lab.

## Can I still get pregnant?

Most likely, yes, especially if the infection was treated promptly. If you've been trying for 12 months (or 6 months if you're 35 or older), see a clinician.

## Fertility checks after chlamydia

| Test | What it shows |
|---|---|
| HSG (tube X-ray with dye) | Whether the tubes are open |
| Chlamydia antibody blood test | A clue to past infection, used by some fertility clinics |
| Ovulation and hormone tests | Other causes of infertility |
| Semen analysis | Male factor, if relevant |

## Men

Evidence that chlamydia causes male infertility is limited. Epididymitis can occasionally affect sperm. See [complications in men](/chlamydia/complications-in-men/).
