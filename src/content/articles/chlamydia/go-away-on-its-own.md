## Why waiting is risky

| Risk | Why |
|---|---|
| Pelvic inflammatory disease | Infection spreads to the uterus and tubes |
| Infertility and ectopic pregnancy | From tubal scarring |
| Epididymitis | Painful swelling near the testicle |
| Passing it on | Partners can be infected meanwhile |
| Higher HIV risk | Inflammation makes HIV easier to get |

## What to do instead

See a clinician for treatment. Health departments often treat for free. See [getting treated for chlamydia](/chlamydia/treatment/).

If you'd rather test privately, a [lab-processed home kit](/go/k1/) uses the same type of lab test as a clinic.
