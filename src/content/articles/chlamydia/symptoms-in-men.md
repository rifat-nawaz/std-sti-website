## Common symptoms

| Symptom | What it's like |
|---|---|
| Discharge | Clear, white or cloudy; may be a drip or stain in underwear |
| Burning | Stinging when peeing, often at the tip |
| Itching | Irritation inside the penis |
| Testicle pain | Ache or swelling, usually on one side |
| Rectal symptoms | Pain, discharge or bleeding, after receptive anal sex |

If you'd rather test privately, a [lab-processed home kit](/go/k1/) uses the same type of lab test as a clinic.

## How men check

You can't check for chlamydia yourself. A **first-catch urine** test is the usual sample; throat and rectal swabs are needed after oral or anal sex. See [chlamydia testing for men](/chlamydia/testing-for-men/).

## Urgent signs

Sudden, severe testicle pain can be testicular torsion, which is an emergency. Go to an emergency room.

## Could it be something else?

Gonorrhea, *Mycoplasma genitalium*, trichomoniasis and urinary infections cause similar symptoms. Testing finds the cause.
