## Risk by activity

| Activity | Risk |
|---|---|
| Fingering or hand jobs | Low |
| Mutual masturbation | Low |
| Genital rubbing without penetration | Possible |
| Sharing sex toys without cleaning | Possible |
| Touching your eye with infected fluid | Possible eye infection |

## Making toys safer

1. Use a **new condom** on a toy for each partner or each body part.
2. **Wash toys** with warm water and soap, or as the maker instructs, after each use.
3. Don't move a toy from the anus to the vagina without cleaning it or changing the condom.

## When to test

If a partner tests positive after any sexual contact, get tested about 2 weeks later, or right away if you have symptoms. See [when to test](/std-testing/when-to-test/).

If you'd rather test privately, a [lab-processed home kit](/go/k1/) uses the same type of lab test as a clinic.
