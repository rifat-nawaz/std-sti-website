## Possible causes

| Cause | Clues |
|---|---|
| Reinfection | New or untreated partner |
| Another infection | Gonorrhea, *Mycoplasma genitalium*, trichomoniasis, urinary infection |
| Pelvic inflammatory disease | Lower belly pain, pain during sex, fever |
| Epididymitis | Ache or swelling beside a testicle |
| Pelvic floor tension | Ongoing ache with negative tests |

## What to expect at a recheck

- Questions about partners, timing and the type of pain
- Repeat STD tests and a urine test
- A pelvic or testicular exam, and sometimes an ultrasound
- A referral if pain continues with negative tests

## While you wait

- Avoid sex until you know the cause.
- Don't take leftover or online antibiotics.
- Keep a pain diary: when it hurts and what makes it worse. It helps your clinician.
