## Why infections happen with condoms

- The condom went on **after** genital contact began
- It **broke or slipped** off
- It was used for some acts but not others, such as oral sex
- It was **reused** or damaged by oil-based lubricant

Prefer to test at home? A [lab-processed home test](/go/k1/) lets you collect a sample in private and get results online.

## The condom broke: what now?

1. Consider **emergency contraception** if pregnancy is possible. Ask a pharmacist.
2. If HIV exposure is possible, ask about [PEP](/hiv/pep/) within 72 hours.
3. **Test for chlamydia and gonorrhea** about 2 weeks later.

## Getting the most from condoms

- Use a new condom every time, from start to finish.
- Use water- or silicone-based lubricant.
- Check the expiry date.
- Use condoms or dental dams for oral sex too.
