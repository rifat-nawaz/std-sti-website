## How chlamydia spreads

| Route | Risk |
|---|---|
| Vaginal sex | High |
| Anal sex | High, for both partners |
| Oral sex | Lower, but possible |
| Shared sex toys | Possible if not washed or covered |
| Genital-to-genital contact | Possible, even without penetration |
| Birth | Possible if the parent has chlamydia |
| Infected fluid in the eye | Possible |

You can also order a [home test kit](/go/k1/), collect your sample yourself and mail it to a certified lab.

## How it doesn't spread

- Toilet seats, towels or bedding
- Kissing, hugging or holding hands
- Sharing cups, cutlery or food
- Swimming pools or hot tubs
- Needles (it isn't a bloodborne infection)

## Can it appear on its own?

No. Chlamydia always comes from someone who has it. Because it often causes no symptoms, a partner may have had it for a long time without knowing.
