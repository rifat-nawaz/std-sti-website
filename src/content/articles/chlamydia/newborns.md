## Signs in a newborn

| Condition | Typical timing | Signs |
|---|---|---|
| Conjunctivitis | 5 to 12 days after birth | Swollen eyelids, discharge, red eye |
| Pneumonia | 1 to 3 months | Repetitive cough, fast breathing, no fever |

## What to do

- Tell the pediatrician about chlamydia during pregnancy, even if you were treated.
- Take your baby to be seen promptly if you notice these signs.
- Seek urgent care for breathing problems.

## Prevention

[Testing during pregnancy](/chlamydia/pregnancy/) and treating parents and partners before birth.
