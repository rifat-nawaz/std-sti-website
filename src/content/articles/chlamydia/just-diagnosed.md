## Day-by-day checklist

| When | Do this |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Book a clinician or telehealth visit; ask about partner treatment |
| Day 1 to 2 | Start treatment exactly as prescribed |
| Day 1 to 3 | Tell partners from the last 60 days |
| Day 1 onward | No sex, including oral, until 7 days after treatment ends and partners are treated |
| This week | Test for gonorrhea, HIV and syphilis if not done |
| In 3 months | Retest for chlamydia |

Prefer to test at home? A [lab-processed home test](/go/k1/) lets you collect a sample in private and get results online.

## What you don't need to do

- Wash or throw away sheets, towels or clothes
- Avoid sharing toilets, cups or food
- Stop using tampons or pads
