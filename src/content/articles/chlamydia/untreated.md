## Possible complications

| Who | Complication |
|---|---|
| People with a uterus | PID, tubal scarring, infertility, ectopic pregnancy, chronic pelvic pain |
| People with a penis | Epididymitis (testicle pain and swelling) |
| Anyone | Reactive arthritis, higher HIV risk, eye infection |
| Newborns | Eye infection and pneumonia if born to an infected parent |

## Timeline

There's no fixed timeline. Chlamydia can spread to the upper reproductive tract within weeks, or stay in the cervix for months. That uncertainty is why every infection should be treated promptly.

## Can untreated chlamydia kill you?

Chlamydia itself is very rarely life-threatening. An ectopic pregnancy caused by tubal scarring can be, and needs emergency care.
