## Typical turnaround

| Where | Time |
|---|---|
| Clinic, lab NAAT | 1 to 3 days |
| Clinic, rapid molecular test | Same visit |
| Home kit, mailed to lab | 2 to 5 days after the lab receives it |
| Home molecular test | About 30 minutes |

## Why results can take longer

- Shipping delays or weekends
- Sample needed retesting
- Lab backlogs

## No news is good news?

Not always. Some clinics only call with positives, but results can also get lost. If you haven't heard in a week, call.
