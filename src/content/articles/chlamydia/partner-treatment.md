## How EPT works

1. Your clinician diagnoses you.
2. They give you a prescription or medicine for your partner, with instructions.
3. Your partner takes it without a separate visit.

Prefer to test at home? A [lab-processed home test](/go/k1/) lets you collect a sample in private and get results online.

## Where it's legal

The CDC reports EPT is permissible in nearly every US state. Rules vary; your clinician will know your state's.

## If a partner refuses

- Avoid sex with them until they're treated.
- Ask the health department to contact them.
- Use condoms if you do have sex, and retest at 3 months.
