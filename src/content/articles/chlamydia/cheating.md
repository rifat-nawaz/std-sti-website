## Why it may not mean cheating

| Reason | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Silent infection | Most people have no symptoms |
| Long duration | Infections can last months or longer |
| Earlier negative test | May have been too soon after exposure, or the wrong site |
| No routine testing | Many people have never been tested |

## When it's harder to explain

If both partners had confirmed negative tests at all relevant sites after their last other partner, a new infection suggests a new exposure. A clinician can help you interpret the timeline.

## Next steps

1. Both get tested and treated.
2. Avoid sex until both have finished treatment.
3. Talk when you're both calm. Read [how to tell a partner](/chlamydia/telling-a-partner/).
