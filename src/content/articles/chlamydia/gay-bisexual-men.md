## Testing schedule

| Situation | How often |
|---|---|
| Sexually active | At least yearly |
| Multiple or anonymous partners, group sex, or partners who do | Every 3 to 6 months |
| Living with HIV | At least yearly, more often if at risk |
| On PrEP | At PrEP visits |

## Which sites

- **Urine:** for urethral infection
- **Rectal swab:** after receptive anal sex
- **Throat swab:** not routine for chlamydia, but recommended for gonorrhea; ask

## Prevention

Condoms, regular testing, [PrEP](/hiv/prep/), and asking a clinician about prevention options after sex if you've had a bacterial STD recently.
