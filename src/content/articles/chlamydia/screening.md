## Screening recommendations

| Who | How often |
|---|---|
| Sexually active women 24 and under | Every year |
| Women 25 and older at higher risk | Every year |
| Pregnant people 24 and under, or at risk | First prenatal visit, and again in the third trimester if risk continues |
| Gay and bisexual men | At least yearly (urine and rectal); every 3 to 6 months if at higher risk |
| People with HIV | At least yearly |
| Anyone treated for chlamydia | Retest about 3 months later |

For privacy and convenience, an [at-home test kit](/go/k1/) processed by a certified lab is another option.

## What about men?

The USPSTF found too little evidence to recommend routine screening of all men. Men with symptoms, a partner with chlamydia, or new partners should still test.

## Teens

Every state allows minors to consent to STD testing, though some set a minimum age.
