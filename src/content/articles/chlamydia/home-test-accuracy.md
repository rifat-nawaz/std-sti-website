## What makes a home test accurate

| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| NAAT processed in a certified lab | Same test clinics use |
| Correct sample | First-catch urine or a vaginal swab, collected as instructed |
| Right timing | About 2 weeks after exposure |
| Fast shipping | Samples should reach the lab promptly |

## FDA-authorized options

- **Home sample collection** for chlamydia and gonorrhea (authorized 2023)
- **A home test with results at home** for chlamydia, gonorrhea and trichomoniasis in people with a vagina (authorized 2025)

## Tests to avoid

Cheap "rapid" chlamydia antigen tests sold online without FDA authorization can miss infections and give unclear lines.
