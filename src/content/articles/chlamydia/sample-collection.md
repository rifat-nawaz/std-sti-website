## Before you start

- Read the whole instruction leaflet first.
- Wash and dry your hands.
- Check the expiry date and the collection days your kit allows for mailing.

## First-catch urine

1. Don't pee for at least 1 hour before.
2. Pee the **first** part of the stream into the cup, as much as the kit asks for.
3. Finish peeing into the toilet.
4. Transfer urine to the tube up to the fill line.

## Vaginal swab

1. Hold the swab by the handle, without touching the tip.
2. Insert about 2 inches into the vagina.
3. Gently rotate for 10 to 30 seconds.
4. Place in the tube and snap off the handle at the line.

## Throat swab

Rub the swab firmly over both tonsil areas and the back of the throat. Avoid eating, drinking or mouthwash for 1 hour before.

## Rectal swab

Insert about 1 to 1.5 inches into the anus and rotate gently for 10 seconds.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Midstream urine | Collect the first part of the stream |
| Peed too recently | Wait at least an hour |
| Toilet water in the cup | Collect directly into the cup |
| Tube overfilled or underfilled | Fill to the line |
| Missed the mailing window | Mail the same day you collect |
