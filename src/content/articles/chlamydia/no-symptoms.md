## Why chlamydia is so often silent

The inflammation chlamydia causes is often mild, and the cervix and rectum have few pain nerves. Many infections simply don't produce anything you'd notice.

## Can silent chlamydia spread?

Yes. You can pass it on whether or not you have symptoms.

## Can silent chlamydia cause damage?

Yes. Chlamydia can move into the uterus and fallopian tubes without obvious symptoms, causing scarring that affects fertility.

## Who should test without symptoms

| Who | How often |
|---|---|
| Sexually active women 24 and under | Every year |
| Women 25+ with a new or multiple partners | Every year |
| Gay and bisexual men | At least yearly, every site used for sex |
| Anyone with a new partner | Before or after the new relationship |
| Pregnant people | At the first prenatal visit if at risk |
