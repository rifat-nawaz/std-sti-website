## Prevention checklist

| Step | Why |
|---|---|
| Condoms for vaginal and anal sex | Block contact with infected fluids |
| Condoms or dental dams for oral sex | Lower throat and genital risk |
| Test regularly | Finds silent infections |
| Test before a new relationship | Both partners start clear |
| Treat partners after a diagnosis | Prevents ping-pong reinfection |
| Cover or wash shared sex toys | Prevents passing fluids |

Prefer to test at home? A [lab-processed home test](/go/k1/) lets you collect a sample in private and get results online.
