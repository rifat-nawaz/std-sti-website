## How they compare

| Clue | More likely UTI | More likely chlamydia |
|---|---|---|
| Peeing often, urgently | Yes | Sometimes |
| Burning when peeing | Yes | Yes |
| Discharge from penis or vagina | No | Often |
| Bleeding after sex or between periods | No | Possible |
| Cloudy or smelly urine | Yes | Less common |
| New sexual partner recently | Not required | Common |

## Why a UTI test can miss chlamydia

A urinalysis or urine culture looks for bacteria that cause bladder infections. Chlamydia needs a specific **NAAT** test. If you're sexually active, ask: "Can you also test for chlamydia and gonorrhea?"

If you'd rather test privately, a [lab-processed home kit](/go/k1/) uses the same type of lab test as a clinic.

## When to suspect an STD

- Symptoms after a new partner
- Discharge
- Symptoms that don't improve after UTI treatment
- A negative urine culture but ongoing burning
