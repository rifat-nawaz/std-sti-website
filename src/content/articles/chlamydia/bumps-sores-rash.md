## What bumps and sores more likely mean

| What you see | More likely cause |
|---|---|
| Painful blisters that break into sores | Herpes |
| Firm, round, painless sore | Syphilis |
| Soft, flesh-colored bumps | Genital warts (HPV) |
| Small red bumps around hair follicles | Ingrown hairs or folliculitis |
| Rash on palms and soles | Secondary syphilis |

## The exception: LGV

Lymphogranuloma venereum, a less common type of chlamydia, can start with a small painless sore, followed by swollen glands or rectal symptoms. See [LGV](/chlamydia/lgv/).

## What to do

- See a clinician, ideally while the sore is fresh, so it can be swabbed.
- Ask for a full panel: herpes swab, syphilis blood test, chlamydia and gonorrhea.
