## Talking about testing with a new partner

Try: "Before we have sex, I'd like us both to get tested. When were you last checked?"

## When to share your history

You don't have to, but some people choose to share it as part of a broader talk about sexual health. Do what feels right.

## Feeling anxious after an STD

It's common to feel embarrassed or worried. Remember chlamydia is very common, curable and doesn't define you. See [coping with STD anxiety](/guides/std-anxiety/) for support.
