## Options during your period

| Sample | Works on your period? |
|---|---|
| Urine | Yes, generally |
| Self-collected vaginal swab | Often; check the kit |
| Clinician-collected swab | Usually yes |

## Tips

- Remove tampons, pads or cups first.
- Follow the kit's specific advice about menstrual blood.
- If you have symptoms, test now rather than waiting.

## Best time to test

About **2 weeks** after a possible exposure, whatever the day of your cycle. See [when to test for chlamydia](/chlamydia/when-to-test/).
