## Side by side

| | At-home lab test | Clinic |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy | High | Varies; insurance statements may show visits |
| Accuracy | High (same NAAT) | High |
| Cost | Paid out of pocket, usually | Often covered or free |
| Results | A few days after the lab receives it | 1 to 3 days |
| Treatment | Via telehealth if included | Often same visit |
| Exam for symptoms | No | Yes |

Prefer to test at home? A [lab-processed home test](/go/k1/) lets you collect a sample in private and get results online.

## Choose a clinic if

- You have pain, fever, sores or heavy discharge
- You're pregnant
- A partner tested positive and you want treatment now
- You can't afford a kit; see [free testing](/std-testing/free-testing/)

## Choose a home kit if

- You want routine screening
- Privacy or time is a barrier
- You're comfortable collecting a sample yourself
