## Why chlamydia comes back

- A partner wasn't treated
- Sex before you and your partner finished treatment and waited
- A new partner with chlamydia

## How to avoid getting it again

1. Make sure every partner is treated.
2. Wait until 7 days after you **and** your partners finish treatment before sex.
3. Use condoms with new or untested partners.
4. Retest about 3 months after treatment.
