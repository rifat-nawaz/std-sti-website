## What affects the risk

| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Vaginal or anal sex | Higher risk than oral sex |
| No condom | Higher risk |
| Partner has symptoms or a recent infection | Possibly higher risk |
| Repeated sex with an infected partner | Risk adds up over time |

## Why there's no single number

Studies of per-act risk are limited, and estimates vary widely. What's clear: many people get chlamydia from a partner after only a few encounters, and some after just one.

## What to do

- **Test about 2 weeks** after the encounter. See [how soon after sex to test](/std-testing/when-to-test/).
- If the partner has tested positive, see a clinician. You may be treated without waiting.
