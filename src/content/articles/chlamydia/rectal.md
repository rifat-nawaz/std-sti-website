## Symptoms

- Rectal pain or discomfort
- Discharge or mucus
- Bleeding
- Feeling of needing to go when you don't
- Often, nothing at all

For privacy and convenience, an [at-home test kit](/go/k1/) processed by a certified lab is another option.

## Who needs a rectal swab

- Anyone who has receptive anal sex
- Gay and bisexual men: at least yearly, more often if at higher risk
- People with rectal symptoms
- Women, if a clinician advises it

## How to self-collect

Insert the swab about 1 to 1.5 inches into the anus, rotate gently for 10 seconds, and place it in the tube. Follow your kit's exact instructions.

## Only the rectal swab was positive?

That's common. Each site is tested separately. Treatment is chosen by a clinician; ask whether you need a follow-up test.
