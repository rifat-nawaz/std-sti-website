## Giving vs receiving

| Act | What can happen |
|---|---|
| Giving oral sex to a penis | The throat can become infected |
| Giving oral sex to a vulva | Possible, though less studied |
| Receiving oral sex | Genital infection from a partner's throat is possible but less common |

## Symptoms

Most throat infections cause no symptoms. A few people get a mild sore throat. See [chlamydia in the throat](/chlamydia/throat/) for more.

## Testing after oral sex

- Ask for a **throat swab**. A urine test won't find it.
- Test about **2 weeks** after the exposure.
- Many home kits offer a throat swab as an add-on.

## Lowering your risk

- Use condoms for oral sex on a penis.
- Use a dental dam for oral sex on a vulva or anus.
- Test regularly if you have new or multiple partners.
