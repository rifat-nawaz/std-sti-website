## What it can look like

| | People with a penis | People with a vagina |
|---|---|---|
| Color | Clear, white or cloudy | Cloudy, white or yellowish |
| Amount | A small drip or underwear stain | More than usual |
| Smell | Usually none | Usually none |

## Other causes of discharge

| Discharge | More likely cause |
|---|---|
| Fishy smell, thin, gray | BV |
| Frothy, yellow-green | Trichomoniasis |
| Thick, white, clumpy, itchy | Yeast infection |
| Thick, yellow or green from the penis | Gonorrhea |

## What to do

Get tested for chlamydia, gonorrhea and trichomoniasis, plus BV and yeast if you have a vagina. See [chlamydia vs yeast infection vs BV](/chlamydia/vs-yeast-infection-bv/).
