## Right now

1. **Don't have sex** until you've been treated.
2. **Contact a clinician** or health department. Say: "My partner tested positive for chlamydia."
3. **Ask about expedited partner therapy** if your partner's clinician offers it.

## Testing

- Test now if you have symptoms.
- Test at about 2 weeks after the last exposure for a reliable result.
- You may be treated before results; that's standard practice.

## Is it too early for treatment?

No. Clinicians treat exposed partners promptly because waiting risks complications and spread.
