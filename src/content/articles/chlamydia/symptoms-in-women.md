## Common symptoms

| Symptom | Notes |
|---|---|
| Discharge | More than usual, cloudy or yellowish |
| Burning when peeing | Often mild |
| Bleeding | After sex, or spotting between periods |
| Pain during sex | Deep pain can mean the cervix is inflamed |
| Lower belly pain | Can be a sign of PID |

## Does chlamydia make you feel sick or tired?

Usually not. Fever, nausea or feeling unwell alongside pelvic pain can mean the infection has spread to the uterus or tubes. Get seen the same day.

## Could it be something else?

BV, yeast infections, trichomoniasis and urinary infections cause similar symptoms. See [chlamydia vs yeast infection vs BV](/chlamydia/vs-yeast-infection-bv/).

## What to do

Get a vaginal swab test, which you can often collect yourself. It's more accurate than urine for women.

You can also order a [home test kit](/go/k1/), collect your sample yourself and mail it to a certified lab.
