## Why chlamydia comes back

1. **An untreated partner.** The most common cause.
2. **Sex too soon**, before you or your partner finished treatment and waited 7 days.
3. **A new partner** with chlamydia.
4. **Treatment not completed** as prescribed.
5. **Rarely**, an infection that persists despite treatment.

## Was it a real positive?

If you tested within about **3 weeks of treatment**, the test may have detected genetic material from dead bacteria. Your clinician will decide whether to retest or treat again.

## How to break the cycle

- Get treated again by a clinician.
- Make sure **every partner** is tested and treated. Ask about expedited partner therapy.
- Avoid sex until 7 days after you **and** your partners finish treatment.
- Retest about 3 months later.

## When it's not chlamydia

If chlamydia tests are negative but symptoms continue, ask about testing for *Mycoplasma genitalium*, trichomoniasis and gonorrhea. See [Mycoplasma genitalium](/mycoplasma-genitalium/).
