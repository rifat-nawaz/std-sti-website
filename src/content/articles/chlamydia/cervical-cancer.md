## What the research shows

HPV is the cause of nearly all cervical cancers. Chlamydia has been linked in some studies to a higher chance of HPV persisting, possibly because of inflammation, but it is not a cause on its own.

## Chlamydia and an abnormal Pap

Inflammation from chlamydia can sometimes lead to an abnormal or unclear Pap result. Your clinician may repeat the test after the infection is treated.

## What to do

- Keep up with [cervical screening](/hpv/).
- Get the [HPV vaccine](/hpv/vaccine/) if you're eligible.
- Test for chlamydia regularly if you're at risk.
