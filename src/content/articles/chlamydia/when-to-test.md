## Timing at a glance

| When you test | What it means |
|---|---|
| Within 3 days | Too early to detect an infection from this exposure |
| 7 days | May detect it, but a negative should be repeated |
| 14 days or later | Reliable for most people |

## Symptoms? Don't wait

If you have symptoms, test right away, whatever the date.

## Partner tested positive?

See a clinician now. You may be treated before testing, or tested and treated the same day.

Check your dates with the [window period calculator](/std-testing/window-period/).

You can also order a [home test kit](/go/k1/), collect your sample yourself and mail it to a certified lab.
