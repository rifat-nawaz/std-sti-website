## Retesting timeline

| Situation | When to test |
|---|---|
| Everyone treated for chlamydia | About 3 months after treatment |
| Can't make it at 3 months | At your next visit within 12 months |
| Pregnant | About 4 weeks after treatment, then again within 3 months |
| Symptoms that won't clear | Talk to your clinician now |

## Test of cure vs retest

- **Test of cure:** checks that treatment worked. Usually only needed during pregnancy or when symptoms continue.
- **Retest at 3 months:** checks for a **new** infection. Recommended for everyone.

## Why not sooner?

Within about 3 weeks of treatment, a NAAT can still pick up genetic material from bacteria that are already dead. That can lead to a confusing positive.

## If the retest is positive

That usually means reinfection. See [chlamydia came back?](/chlamydia/reinfection/) for next steps.
