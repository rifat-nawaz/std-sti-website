## Warning signs

- Sharp pain on one side of the belly or pelvis
- Vaginal bleeding
- Shoulder-tip pain
- Dizziness or fainting

**Call 911 or go to an emergency room** if you have these with a positive pregnancy test or a missed period.

## If you've had chlamydia or PID

Tell your clinician as soon as you know you're pregnant. They may arrange an early ultrasound.

## Prevention

Test regularly and treat chlamydia early. See [who should get screened](/chlamydia/screening/).
