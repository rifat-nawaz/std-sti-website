## Risks

| For | Possible effects |
|---|---|
| Pregnancy | Preterm labor, premature rupture of membranes |
| Baby | Eye infection, pneumonia |

## Testing schedule

| When | Who |
|---|---|
| First prenatal visit | Under 25, or 25+ at higher risk |
| Third trimester | If under 25 or still at risk |
| About 4 weeks after treatment | Everyone treated (test of cure) |
| Within 3 months of treatment | Retest |

Prefer to test at home? A [lab-processed home test](/go/k1/) lets you collect a sample in private and get results online.

## What to expect

- Your prenatal provider prescribes a treatment that's safe in pregnancy.
- Avoid sex until you and your partner have finished treatment.
- Ask about breastfeeding if you have questions; treatment is compatible with feeding plans in most cases.
