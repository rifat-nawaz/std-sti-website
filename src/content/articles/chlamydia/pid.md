## Signs of PID

- Pain in the lower belly or pelvis
- Pain during deep sex
- Fever or chills
- Unusual discharge or bleeding
- Burning when peeing

## Go to an emergency room if you have

- Severe belly pain
- Fever above 101°F with pain
- Vomiting and can't keep fluids down
- Pain with a positive pregnancy test

## What to expect from care

A clinician examines you, tests for STDs and pregnancy, and may order an ultrasound. Urgent care and emergency rooms can diagnose PID. Treatment is chosen by the clinician; some people need hospital care.

## Partners

Partners from the last 60 days need testing and treatment, even if your tests were negative.
