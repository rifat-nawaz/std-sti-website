## Common causes

| Cause | Clues |
|---|---|
| Reinfection | Sex with a partner who wasn't treated, or a new partner |
| *Mycoplasma genitalium* | Burning or discharge that won't clear despite negative chlamydia and gonorrhea tests |
| Trichomoniasis | Itching, frothy discharge, fishy smell |
| Yeast infection | Itching and thick white discharge, often after antibiotics |
| Bacterial vaginosis | Thin gray discharge with a fishy smell |
| Urinary tract infection | Frequent, urgent peeing |
| Slow healing | Mild irritation that keeps getting better |

## What your clinician may do

- Ask about partners and whether treatment was finished
- Retest for chlamydia, usually at least 3 weeks after treatment
- Test for gonorrhea, trichomoniasis, *Mycoplasma genitalium* and urinary infections
- Examine you for signs of pelvic inflammatory disease or epididymitis

## What to avoid

- Taking leftover, borrowed or online medicine
- Having sex until you know what's going on
- Douching or using scented products, which can make irritation worse

See [chlamydia came back?](/chlamydia/reinfection/) if a test is positive again.
