## Result by result

| Result | Meaning | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| Detected / Positive | Chlamydia found | See a clinician; tell partners |
| Not detected / Negative | Not found in this sample | Retest if you tested early or have symptoms |
| Equivocal / Indeterminate | Unclear | Retest |
| Invalid / QNS | Sample couldn't be tested | Collect a new sample |

## After a positive result

1. [Get treated](/chlamydia/treatment/)
2. Tell partners from the last 60 days
3. Avoid sex until treatment is finished and partners are treated
4. [Retest](/chlamydia/retesting/) in about 3 months

## After a negative result

Retest at 2 weeks if your test was early. If symptoms continue, see [negative test but symptoms](/chlamydia/negative-test-symptoms/).
