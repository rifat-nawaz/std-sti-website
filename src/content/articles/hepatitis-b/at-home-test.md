## How we compare tests

We compare features you can check: which markers are tested, lab certification, support and privacy. We haven't lab-tested products ourselves.

If you'd rather test privately, a [lab-processed home kit](/go/k2/) uses the same type of lab test as a clinic.

## What to look for

| Feature | Why |
|---|---|
| HBsAg | Current infection |
| Anti-HBs | Immunity |
| Anti-HBc | Past infection |
| Clinician follow-up | Next steps after a positive |
