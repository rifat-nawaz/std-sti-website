## Your monitoring tests

| Test | What it shows |
|---|---|
| ALT (liver enzyme) | Whether the liver is inflamed |
| HBV DNA (viral load) | How much virus is in your blood |
| HBeAg and anti-HBe | How active the virus is |
| Liver scan or elastography | Scarring (fibrosis) in the liver |
| Ultrasound | Early signs of liver cancer, for people at higher risk |

## Who needs medicine?

A specialist looks at your viral load, liver enzymes and liver scarring. People with active liver inflammation or scarring are more likely to need medicine. Others are monitored and may never need it.

## Everyday precautions

- **Avoid alcohol**, which speeds up liver damage.
- **Check with your clinician** before any new medicine, herbal product or supplement.
- **Get vaccinated against hepatitis A.**
- **Keep a healthy weight.** Fatty liver adds to liver damage.
- **Don't share** razors, toothbrushes or needles. Cover cuts.

## Protecting others

Partners and household members should be tested and vaccinated. Use condoms until partners are protected. See the [hepatitis B vaccine](/hepatitis-b/vaccine/).
