## Protecting your baby

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| Early pregnancy | Hepatitis B test |
| Late pregnancy | Care if viral load is high |
| Within 12 hours of birth | Preventive shots for the baby |
| Infancy | Complete vaccine series, then testing |
