## Who should test

| Who | How often |
|---|---|
| Every adult | At least once |
| Pregnant people | Every pregnancy |
| People at ongoing risk | Regularly |
| Partners and household of people with hepatitis B | Now |

You can also order a [home test kit](/go/k1/), collect your sample yourself and mail it to a certified lab.
