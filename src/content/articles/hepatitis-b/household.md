## Safe vs avoid

| Safe | Avoid sharing |
|---|---|
| Hugging, kissing on the cheek | Razors |
| Sharing meals and dishes | Toothbrushes |
| Shared bathrooms | Nail clippers |
| Laundry together | Glucose meters or needles |
