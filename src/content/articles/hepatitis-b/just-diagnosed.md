## First steps

1. Book a specialist appointment.
2. Get monitoring tests: liver enzymes, viral load, HBeAg, liver scan.
3. Tell partners and household; get them tested and vaccinated.
4. Avoid alcohol and check supplements.
5. Get the hepatitis A vaccine.

See [chronic hepatitis B care](/hepatitis-b/treatment/).
