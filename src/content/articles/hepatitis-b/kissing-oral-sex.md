## Risk at a glance

| Activity | Risk |
|---|---|
| Sharing food, cups, utensils | None |
| Casual kissing | None |
| Deep kissing | Very low unless bleeding |
| Oral sex | Some risk |
| Vaginal or anal sex | Higher risk |
