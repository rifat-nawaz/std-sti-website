## Who should get it

| Group | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Babies and children | Routine vaccination |
| Adults 19 to 59 | Everyone who isn't already protected |
| Adults 60 and older | Those with risk factors; anyone else who wants it |
| Partners and household of someone with hepatitis B | Strongly recommended |

## Checking if you're protected

An **anti-HBs blood test** shows whether you're immune, from vaccination or past infection. The CDC's triple-panel hepatitis B test, recommended once for every adult, includes it.

Some people are checked after vaccination to confirm it worked, including health care workers, partners of people with hepatitis B, people on dialysis and people with HIV.

## How long protection lasts

For people with a healthy immune system, protection from a completed series lasts for decades, likely for life.

## Pregnancy

Vaccination during pregnancy is recommended for those who aren't protected. Ask your provider which vaccine is right for you.
