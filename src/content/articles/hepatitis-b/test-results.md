## Result patterns

| HBsAg | Anti-HBc | Anti-HBs | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Negative | Negative | Negative | Not protected; get vaccinated |
| Negative | Negative | Positive | Immune from vaccine |
| Negative | Positive | Positive | Past infection, immune |
| Positive | Positive | Negative | Current infection |
| Negative | Positive | Negative | Isolated core antibody; needs follow-up |
