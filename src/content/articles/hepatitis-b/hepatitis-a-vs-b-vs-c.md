## Side by side

| | Hepatitis A | Hepatitis B | Hepatitis C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main spread | Food, water, oral-anal contact | Sex, blood, birth | Blood |
| Chronic? | No | Sometimes | Often |
| Vaccine | Yes | Yes | No |
| Curable? | Clears on its own | Acute often clears; chronic managed | Yes |
