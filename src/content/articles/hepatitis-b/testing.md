## The triple panel

| Test | Shows |
|---|---|
| HBsAg | Current infection |
| Anti-HBs | Immunity |
| Total anti-HBc | Past or current infection |

See [how to read results](/hepatitis-b/test-results/).

For privacy and convenience, an [at-home test kit](/go/k1/) processed by a certified lab is another option.
