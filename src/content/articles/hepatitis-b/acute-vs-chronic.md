## Chance of chronic infection

| Age at infection | Becomes chronic |
|---|---|
| Infants | About 90% |
| Young children | 25% to 50% |
| Adults | About 5% |

You can also order a [home test kit](/go/k1/), collect your sample yourself and mail it to a certified lab.

## How you find out

HBsAg still positive after 6 months means chronic infection. See [reading hepatitis B results](/hepatitis-b/test-results/).
