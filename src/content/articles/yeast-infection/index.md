## Symptoms

- Itching and irritation of the vagina and vulva
- Thick, white discharge that may look like cottage cheese, usually without a strong smell
- Burning, redness or swelling
- Pain when peeing or during sex

## Yeast infection or STD?

| Clue | Points to |
|---|---|
| Fishy smell | BV or trichomoniasis |
| Frothy yellow-green discharge | Trichomoniasis |
| Blisters or open sores | Herpes |
| Discharge with burning when peeing, after a new partner | Chlamydia or gonorrhea |
| Strong itch with thick white discharge, no smell | Yeast infection |

These clues aren't reliable on their own. Many people who think they have a yeast infection have something else.

## Treatment

For a typical, uncomplicated yeast infection:

- **Over-the-counter vaginal antifungals** such as clotrimazole or miconazole, for 1 to 7 days
- **Prescription fluconazole**, 150 mg as a single pill

During pregnancy, only vaginal treatments for 7 days are recommended.

## When to see a clinician

- It's your first yeast infection
- You're pregnant
- Symptoms don't clear after treatment
- You have four or more infections in a year
- You have a new partner and could have an STD

## Prevention

- Wear breathable underwear and change out of wet clothes promptly.
- Avoid douching and scented products.
- If you have diabetes, keep blood sugar in range.
