## Yeast or something else?

| Sign | More likely |
|---|---|
| Itchy red rash, white patches | Yeast |
| Blisters or sores | Herpes |
| Discharge from the urethra | Gonorrhea, chlamydia, trich |
| Painless sore | Syphilis |
