## Possible long-term effects

| Area | Effects |
|---|---|
| Brain and nerves | Neurosyphilis: memory loss, personality change, paralysis |
| Eyes | Ocular syphilis: vision loss |
| Ears | Otosyphilis: hearing loss, ringing |
| Heart and blood vessels | Aortic damage |
| Skin and bone | Destructive lesions (gummas) |
| Pregnancy | Stillbirth, infant death, lifelong problems |
