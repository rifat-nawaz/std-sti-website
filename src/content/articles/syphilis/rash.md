## Secondary symptoms

| Symptom | Notes |
|---|---|
| Rash | Palms, soles, trunk; usually not itchy |
| Fever, tiredness, aches | Flu-like |
| Swollen glands | Neck, armpits, groin |
| Patchy hair loss | Scalp, eyebrows, beard |
| Mouth or genital patches | Grayish-white, moist |
| Wart-like growths | In warm, moist areas (condyloma lata) |
