## What it feels like

- Fever and chills
- Headache
- Muscle or joint aches
- Sometimes a temporary flare of the rash

It usually starts within a few hours of the first treatment and fades within a day.

## What to do

- Rest and drink fluids.
- Ask a pharmacist or your clinician what's safe to take for fever.
- Keep your next appointment and follow-up blood tests.

## Reaction vs allergy

| Jarisch-Herxheimer reaction | Allergic reaction |
|---|---|
| Fever, chills, aches | Hives, swelling, trouble breathing |
| Within 24 hours, then passes | Can be sudden and severe |
| Not dangerous for most people | Call 911 if breathing is affected |
