## Common result combinations

| Treponemal | RPR / VDRL | Usually means |
|---|---|---|
| Non-reactive | Non-reactive | No syphilis (or too early) |
| Reactive | Reactive (titer) | Current or recent infection |
| Reactive | Non-reactive | Past treated infection, or very early infection |
| Non-reactive | Reactive | Likely false-positive RPR |

For privacy and convenience, an [at-home test kit](/go/k1/) processed by a certified lab is another option.

## Titers explained

| Titer | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1:1 to 1:4 | Low |
| 1:8 to 1:16 | Moderate |
| 1:32 and higher | High |

Titers are compared over time, not judged alone. See [follow-up blood tests](/syphilis/follow-up-rpr/).
