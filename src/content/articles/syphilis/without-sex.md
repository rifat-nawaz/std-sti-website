## No risk

Toilet seats, doorknobs, swimming pools, hot tubs, shared bathrooms, clothes, cups, utensils, hugging.

## Rare risk

- Direct contact between broken skin and an open sore
- Shared needles
- Blood transfusion (screened in the US)

## Positive test with no sexual contact?

A first screening test can be falsely positive. A confirmatory test will clarify. See [syphilis false positive](/syphilis/false-positive/).
