## Signs

- Painless sore on the vulva, in the vagina, on the cervix, anus or mouth
- Swollen glands in the groin
- Rash on palms and soles
- Fever, sore throat, tiredness
- Patchy hair loss

See [syphilis during pregnancy](/syphilis/pregnancy/).
