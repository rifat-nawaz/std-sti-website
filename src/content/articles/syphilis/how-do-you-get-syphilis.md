## Routes

| Route | Risk |
|---|---|
| Vaginal, anal or oral sex with a sore or rash present | High |
| Kissing someone with a mouth sore | Possible |
| Pregnancy and birth | High if untreated |
| Touching a sore with broken skin | Possible |
| Sharing needles | Rare |

## Not a risk

Toilet seats, doorknobs, pools, hot tubs, clothes, shared dishes.
