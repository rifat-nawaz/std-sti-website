## Who and how often

| Who | How often |
|---|---|
| Everyone who is pregnant | First prenatal visit; often again at about 28 weeks and at delivery |
| Gay and bisexual men | At least yearly; every 3 to 6 months if at higher risk |
| People with HIV | At least yearly |
| People on PrEP | At PrEP visits |
| Others at higher risk | Regularly, based on risk |
