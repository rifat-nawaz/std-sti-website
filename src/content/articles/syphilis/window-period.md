## Is my test conclusive?

| Weeks after exposure | Result |
|---|---|
| Under 3 | Too early |
| 6 | Often reliable; repeat at 12 if negative |
| 8 | More reliable; repeat at 12 if concerned |
| 12 | Conclusive for most people |
