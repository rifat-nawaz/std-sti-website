## Stage by stage

| Stage | When | Signs | Contagious through sex? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | 10 to 90 days after exposure (average 3 weeks) | Firm, painless sore; heals in 3 to 6 weeks | Yes |
| Secondary | Weeks to a few months later | Rash (often palms and soles), fever, swollen glands, hair loss, mouth patches | Yes |
| Early latent | Within 1 year of infection | None | Possible, if symptoms return |
| Late latent | More than 1 year | None | Unlikely |
| Tertiary | 10 to 30 years | Heart, brain, nerve, bone damage | No, but harmful |

## Can it skip stages?

Stages can overlap, and many people never notice the sore or the rash. Infection of the brain, eyes or ears can happen at **any** stage.

## Pregnancy

Syphilis can pass to a baby at any stage. See [syphilis during pregnancy](/syphilis/pregnancy/).
