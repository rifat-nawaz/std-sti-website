## What the stage can tell you

| Stage | Likely infected |
|---|---|
| Primary or secondary | Weeks to months ago |
| Early latent | Within the last year |
| Late latent | More than a year ago, possibly much longer |

See [syphilis false positive](/syphilis/false-positive/).
