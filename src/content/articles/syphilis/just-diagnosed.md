## What happens next

| Step | What to expect |
|---|---|
| Staging | History, exam and blood tests |
| Treatment | Plan chosen by your clinician; may involve more than one visit |
| Health department call | Help notifying partners anonymously |
| HIV test | Recommended for everyone with syphilis |
| Follow-up | Repeat RPR or VDRL at 6 and 12 months (and 24 for latent) |

See [getting treated for syphilis](/syphilis/treatment/).
