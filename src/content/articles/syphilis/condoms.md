## Why condoms aren't enough on their own

Syphilis spreads through skin contact with a sore or rash. A condom only protects the skin it covers.

## Layer your protection

- Condoms and dental dams every time
- No sex if anyone has an unexplained sore or rash
- Regular testing: see [who should get tested](/syphilis/screening/)
