## Sore look-alikes

| Condition | Clues |
|---|---|
| Syphilis chancre | Firm, round, painless, usually single |
| Herpes | Painful clustered blisters |
| Canker sore | Painful, inside the mouth, yellow-white base |
| Ingrown hair | Tender bump with a hair, around hair follicles |
| Friction sore | After rough sex, heals quickly |

## Rash look-alikes

| Condition | Clues |
|---|---|
| Secondary syphilis | Palms and soles, not itchy, with fever or swollen glands |
| Pityriasis rosea | One large patch first, then a "Christmas tree" pattern on the trunk |
| Psoriasis | Thick, silvery scales on elbows, knees, scalp |
| Allergic or drug rash | Itchy; follows a new medicine or product |
