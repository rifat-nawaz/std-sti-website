## Documents to keep

- Diagnosis date and stage
- Treatment dates and clinic details
- All RPR or VDRL titer results

## US immigration exams

Panel physicians test many adult applicants. If you test positive, you'll be evaluated and treated if needed; a properly treated infection is generally not a barrier.

## Blood donation

Blood centers ask about syphilis and usually require a waiting period after completing treatment. Check your blood center's current rules.

## Work visas abroad

Some countries screen workers for syphilis. Rules vary widely; ask the employer or embassy before you travel.
