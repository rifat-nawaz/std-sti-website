## Timeline

| After exposure | What can happen |
|---|---|
| 10 to 90 days (average 21) | First sore |
| 3 to 6 weeks | Blood tests may start to turn positive |
| Up to 12 weeks | Blood tests reliable |
| Weeks to months after the sore | Secondary rash |

See the [window period calculator](/std-testing/window-period/).
