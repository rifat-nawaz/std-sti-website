## Typical healing times after treatment

| Sign | Usually heals in |
|---|---|
| Sore (chancre) | A few weeks |
| Body or palm and sole rash | Several weeks |
| Patchy hair loss | A few months |
| Swollen glands | A few weeks |

## Get rechecked if

- A new sore or rash appears
- Symptoms don't improve within a few weeks
- You develop vision, hearing or nerve symptoms (urgent)

Follow-up blood tests are the real check of a cure. See [follow-up blood tests after syphilis](/syphilis/follow-up-rpr/).
