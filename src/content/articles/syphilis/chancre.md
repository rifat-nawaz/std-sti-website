## What it looks like

- Round or oval
- Firm edges, clean base
- Usually painless
- Often with swollen glands nearby

## Where it appears

| Common | Easy to miss |
|---|---|
| Penis, vulva | Inside the vagina or on the cervix |
| Anus | Inside the rectum |
| Lips, mouth | Throat |

## What to do

Get it checked while it's present. See [syphilis vs herpes](/syphilis/vs-herpes/).
