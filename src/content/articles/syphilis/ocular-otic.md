## Eye warning signs

- Blurry or cloudy vision
- Eye pain or redness
- Floaters or flashes
- Sensitivity to light
- Vision loss

## Ear warning signs

- Sudden hearing loss
- Ringing (tinnitus)
- Dizziness or vertigo

**Get emergency care the same day.**
