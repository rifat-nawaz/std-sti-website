## Why they're linked

- Open sores give HIV an easy way in or out.
- Inflammation draws HIV target cells to the area.
- The same kinds of sexual contact spread both.

## What to do

| Situation | Step |
|---|---|
| Diagnosed with syphilis | Test for HIV now, and again in 3 months |
| HIV-negative and at ongoing risk | Ask about [PrEP](/hiv/prep/) |
| Exposure in the last 72 hours | Ask about [PEP](/hiv/pep/) |
| Living with HIV | Test for syphilis at least yearly |
