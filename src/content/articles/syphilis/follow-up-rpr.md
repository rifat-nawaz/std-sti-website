## Typical follow-up schedule

| Stage | Repeat RPR or VDRL |
|---|---|
| Primary or secondary | 6 and 12 months |
| Latent | 6, 12 and 24 months |
| People with HIV | More often, as advised |
| Pregnancy | As advised by your prenatal provider |

## Reading the titer

A titer like **1:32** means the sample still reacted after being diluted 32 times. Each step down (1:32 → 1:16 → 1:8) is one dilution.

- **Fourfold drop** (two steps, e.g. 1:32 → 1:8): a good response
- **Stays the same or falls less**: your clinician may re-evaluate
- **Fourfold rise** (e.g. 1:4 → 1:16): suggests reinfection or failed treatment

## Tips

- Keep a note of each result with the date and lab.
- Use the same type of test each time.
- Retest sooner if you have new sores, a rash or a new partner with syphilis.
