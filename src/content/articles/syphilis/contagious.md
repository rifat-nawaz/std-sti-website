## Contagiousness by stage

| Stage | Spread through sex | Spread to a baby |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Yes | Yes |
| Secondary | Yes | Yes |
| Early latent | Possible | Yes |
| Late latent | Unlikely | Yes |
| After treatment | No, once healed | Prevented by timely treatment |

See [how long to wait before sex after treatment](/syphilis/sex-after-treatment/).
