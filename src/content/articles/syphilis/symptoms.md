## By stage

| Stage | Symptoms |
|---|---|
| Primary | Firm, round, painless sore on the genitals, anus or mouth |
| Secondary | Rash, fever, swollen glands, sore throat, patchy hair loss, tiredness, mouth patches |
| Latent | None |
| Tertiary | Heart, brain, nerve and organ damage |

## Urgent symptoms at any stage

- Vision changes or eye pain
- Hearing loss or ringing in the ears
- Severe headache, confusion or weakness
