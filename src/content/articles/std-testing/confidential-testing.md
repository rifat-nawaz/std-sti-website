## Most private options

| Option | Privacy |
|---|---|
| Health department clinic | High; often anonymous options |
| Title X family planning clinic | High, including for teens |
| Home kit, self-pay | High; check data policies |
| Doctor with insurance | Statements may show the visit |

Prefer to test at home? A [lab-processed home test](/go/k1/) lets you collect a sample in private and get results online.

## Reportable STDs

Clinicians and labs report some STDs, like chlamydia, gonorrhea, syphilis and HIV, to health departments to track trends and help with partner notification. Reports are confidential.
