## Cost by option

| Option | Typical cost |
|---|---|
| Health department | Free or low |
| Community health center | Sliding scale |
| Planned Parenthood | Sliding scale; takes insurance |
| Doctor with insurance | Often $0 for screening |
| Urgent care | Visit fee plus labs |
| Home kit | Paid upfront; varies by panel |

See [free testing](/std-testing/free-testing/).

Prefer to test at home? A [lab-processed home test](/go/k1/) lets you collect a sample in private and get results online.
