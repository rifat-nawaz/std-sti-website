## What happens

1. Check-in and paperwork
2. Questions: symptoms, partners, types of sex, condom use, past STDs
3. Samples: urine, swabs, blood
4. Exam if you have symptoms
5. Treatment or a plan for results

If you'd rather test privately, a [lab-processed home kit](/go/k1/) uses the same type of lab test as a clinic.

## How to ask

> "I'd like a full STD panel, including throat and rectal swabs, and HIV and syphilis blood tests."
