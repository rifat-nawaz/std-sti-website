## Couple's checklist

1. Agree to test.
2. Test after window periods from previous partners.
3. Share results.
4. Discuss herpes and HPV.
5. Agree on exclusivity before stopping condoms.

See [how to ask a partner to get tested](/guides/asking-partner-to-test/).

For privacy and convenience, an [at-home test kit](/go/k1/) processed by a certified lab is another option.
