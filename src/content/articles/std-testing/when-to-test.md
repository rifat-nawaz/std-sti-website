## Your testing timeline

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| Within 72 hours | PEP if HIV exposure is possible. Emergency contraception if pregnancy is possible |
| 2 weeks | Test for chlamydia, gonorrhea and trichomoniasis |
| 6 weeks | Test for HIV and syphilis |
| 12 weeks | Repeat HIV and syphilis tests if earlier results were negative |

Want exact dates? Enter the day of the exposure in our [window period calculator](/std-testing/window-period/).

If you'd rather test privately, a [lab-processed home kit](/go/k1/) uses the same type of lab test as a clinic.

## Why you can't test straight away

Every test needs the infection to reach a detectable level. Before that, results come back negative even if you're infected. This gap is the **window period**.

## Test the right places

Tell the clinic or choose a kit based on the sex you had:

- **Vaginal or penile sex:** urine or vaginal swab
- **Oral sex:** throat swab
- **Receptive anal sex:** rectal swab

## If a partner tells you they have an STD

Don't wait for the window period. See a clinician. They'll usually treat you right away for chlamydia, gonorrhea or syphilis, and test you at the same visit.
