## Window period chart

"Earliest" is when a test may first pick up an infection. "Reliable" is when a negative result can be trusted for most people.

| Infection | Test | Earliest | Reliable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chlamydia | NAAT (urine or swab) | 1 week | 2 weeks |
| Gonorrhea | NAAT (urine or swab) | 1 week | 2 weeks |
| Trichomoniasis | NAAT (urine or swab) | 1 week | 4 weeks |
| Syphilis | Blood antibody test | 3 weeks | 12 weeks |
| HIV | Lab nucleic acid test (NAT) | 10 days | 33 days |
| HIV | Lab antigen/antibody blood draw | 18 days | 45 days |
| HIV | Finger-prick antigen/antibody | 18 days | 90 days |
| HIV | Antibody test, including oral self-tests | 23 days | 90 days |
| Herpes | IgG blood test | 3 weeks | 16 weeks |
| Hepatitis B | Surface antigen blood test | 1 week | 9 weeks |
| Hepatitis C | Antibody blood test | 4 weeks | 12 weeks |

HIV figures come from the CDC. Other timings reflect typical guidance and can vary from person to person.

## How to use these timings

1. **Note the date** of the exposure.
2. **Test at 2 weeks** for chlamydia, gonorrhea and trichomoniasis.
3. **Test again at 6 to 12 weeks** for HIV and syphilis, or use a lab HIV test at 45 days.
4. **Test the right body sites.** Oral and anal sex need throat and rectal swabs. Urine won't find those infections.

## When to test sooner

Don't wait out the window if you have symptoms such as discharge, burning when you pee, sores or a rash. A clinician can test a sore directly and may treat you the same day.

If your partner has tested positive, get treated even before your own results come back. Clinicians often treat partners right away.

## What a negative result inside the window means

A negative result inside the window period is good news, but not final. It means no infection was found *yet*. Repeat the test once the reliable date has passed.

## Not on this chart

- **HPV:** there's no window-period test. People with a cervix are checked through routine cervical screening.
- **Mycoplasma genitalium:** tested only when symptoms continue after other tests come back negative.
