## What telehealth can do

| Usually online | Usually in person |
|---|---|
| Reviewing home test results | Severe or worsening symptoms |
| Prescribing for many STDs | Infections whose treatment must be given at a clinic |
| Partner care in many states | Sores that need to be swabbed |
| PrEP prescriptions and follow-up | Pregnancy concerns |
| Answering questions | Emergencies |

## How a visit works

1. Fill in a health questionnaire.
2. Share any test results.
3. Talk with a clinician by video, phone or chat.
4. If appropriate, they send a prescription to a pharmacy or tell you where to go in person.

## Before you book

- **Licensing:** the clinician must be licensed in your state.
- **Cost:** check the visit fee and whether insurance is accepted.
- **Privacy:** read how your health data is stored and shared.
- **Pharmacy:** use a licensed US pharmacy.
