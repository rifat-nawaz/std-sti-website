## Start here

- **Just had a possible exposure?** Check your timing with the [window period calculator](/std-testing/window-period/). Exposure to HIV in the last 72 hours? Get [PEP](/hiv/pep/) first.
- **Have symptoms?** Use the [symptom checker](/guides/symptom-checker/) to see which tests to ask for, and test now.
- **Got results?** Learn [how to read your results](/std-testing/reading-results/).
- **Worried about cost?** Find [free and low-cost testing](/std-testing/free-testing/).

## Which tests you need

| Infection | Sample | Who should test |
|---|---|---|
| Chlamydia and gonorrhea | Urine or vaginal swab; throat and rectal swabs if relevant | Sexually active women 24 and under every year; others with risk |
| Syphilis | Blood | People at higher risk; everyone who is pregnant |
| HIV | Blood or oral fluid | Everyone 15 to 65 at least once; more often with risk |
| Trichomoniasis | Urine or vaginal swab | Women with symptoms or risk; women with HIV every year |
| Hepatitis C | Blood | All adults at least once |
| Herpes | Swab of a sore | People with sores; blood tests aren't advised without symptoms |

## Clinic or home test?

**Clinic:** best if you have symptoms, are pregnant, need treatment the same day or want free testing.

**Lab-processed home kit:** private and convenient. You collect a sample at home, mail it to a lab and get results online. A positive result still needs a clinician for treatment, and some kit companies include a telehealth consult.
