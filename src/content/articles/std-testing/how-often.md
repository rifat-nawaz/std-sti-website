## Testing schedule

| Who | How often | What |
|---|---|---|
| Women 24 and under | Yearly | Chlamydia, gonorrhea |
| Women 25+ at risk | Yearly | Chlamydia, gonorrhea |
| Everyone 15 to 65 | At least once | HIV |
| Every adult | At least once | Hepatitis B and C |
| Gay and bisexual men | Yearly, or every 3 to 6 months | HIV, syphilis, chlamydia, gonorrhea at all sites |
| People on PrEP | Every few months | HIV and STDs |
| Pregnant people | Each pregnancy | HIV, syphilis, hepatitis B, and others by risk |

You can also order a [home test kit](/go/k1/), collect your sample yourself and mail it to a certified lab.
