## Who needs which swab

| Sex you've had | Test |
|---|---|
| Vaginal or penile | Urine or vaginal swab |
| Giving oral sex | Throat swab |
| Receptive anal sex | Rectal swab |

See [rectal gonorrhea](/gonorrhea/rectal/) and [throat gonorrhea](/gonorrhea/throat/).
