## What to expect

1. **Confirm the diagnosis** with a NAAT on a vaginal swab or urine.
2. **See a clinician** in person or by telehealth. They prescribe treatment and explain how to take it.
3. **Tell your clinician if you're pregnant** or breastfeeding. Treatment is safe in pregnancy and important for the baby.
4. **Finish all the treatment**, even if symptoms clear quickly.

Prefer to test at home? A [lab-processed home test](/go/k1/) lets you collect a sample in private and get results online.

## Partners

All current partners need treatment, even if their tests are negative or they have no symptoms. Trich often causes no symptoms in men and can pass back and forth.

## After treatment

- **Avoid sex** until you and your partners finish treatment and symptoms are gone.
- **Women: retest within 3 months.** A test can be done as soon as 2 weeks after treatment.
- **Symptoms continue?** Go back. Reinfection is the most likely reason.

## What to avoid

- Douching, which can spread infection higher in the body
- Scented soaps or sprays on the area
- Home remedies and over-the-counter products, which don't treat trich
