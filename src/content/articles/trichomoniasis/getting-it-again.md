## Preventing reinfection

| Step | Why |
|---|---|
| Treat partners together | Stops ping-pong infection |
| Wait before sex | Lets treatment work |
| Clean sex toys | Removes any parasites |
| Retest | Catches reinfection |

For privacy and convenience, an [at-home test kit](/go/k1/) processed by a certified lab is another option.
