## Retesting at a glance

| Who | When |
|---|---|
| Women | Within 3 months of treatment |
| Men | If symptoms continue |
| Pregnant | As your provider advises |

If you'd rather test privately, a [lab-processed home kit](/go/k1/) uses the same type of lab test as a clinic.
