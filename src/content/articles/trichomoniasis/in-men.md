## Testing for men

| Test | Notes |
|---|---|
| Urine NAAT | Recommended |
| Urethral swab | Sometimes used |
| Microscope (wet mount) | Misses most infections in men |

See [partner has trich but I tested negative](/trichomoniasis/partner-has-trich/).

For privacy and convenience, an [at-home test kit](/go/k1/) processed by a certified lab is another option.
