## Symptoms

| Women | Men |
|---|---|
| Frothy yellow-green or gray discharge | Usually none |
| Fishy smell | Itching inside the penis |
| Itching, redness, soreness | Burning after peeing or ejaculating |
| Pain when peeing or during sex | Some discharge |
