## Why trich comes back

1. A partner wasn't treated, or had sex with someone else
2. Sex before treatment was finished
3. Treatment not taken as prescribed
4. Rarely, a strain that doesn't respond to usual treatment

You can also order a [home test kit](/go/k1/), collect your sample yourself and mail it to a certified lab.

## Breaking the cycle

- Treat **all partners at the same time**.
- Avoid sex until everyone has finished and symptoms are gone.
- Retest within 3 months (women).
- Tell your clinician if trich returns after you and your partners were all treated. They may change the plan and can arrange resistance testing.

## Rule out look-alikes

Discharge and itching can also come from [bacterial vaginosis](/bacterial-vaginosis/) or a [yeast infection](/yeast-infection/).
