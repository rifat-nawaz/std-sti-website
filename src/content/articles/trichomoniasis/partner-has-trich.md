## Why partners are treated

| Reason | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Tests miss infections | Especially in men |
| Reinfection | Untreated partners pass it back |
| Few symptoms | Partners often feel fine |

If you'd rather test privately, a [lab-processed home kit](/go/k1/) uses the same type of lab test as a clinic.
