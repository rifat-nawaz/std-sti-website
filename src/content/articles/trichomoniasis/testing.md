## Test types

| Test | Accuracy | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| NAAT | Highest | Vaginal swab or urine |
| Rapid antigen | Good | Results in minutes, in clinic |
| Wet mount | Lower | Misses many infections |
| Culture | Moderate | Used less often now |
