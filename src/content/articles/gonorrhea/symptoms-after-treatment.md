## Possible causes

| Cause | Clues |
|---|---|
| Reinfection | Sex with an untreated or new partner |
| Chlamydia | Often found alongside gonorrhea |
| *Mycoplasma genitalium* | Burning or discharge with negative chlamydia and gonorrhea tests |
| Trichomoniasis | Itching or irritation, often mild in men |
| Resistant gonorrhea | Symptoms persist with no new sexual contact |

If you'd rather test privately, a [lab-processed home kit](/go/k1/) uses the same type of lab test as a clinic.

## What your clinician may do

- Take a **culture** from the infected site so the lab can test which antibiotics work
- Repeat the gonorrhea NAAT
- Test for chlamydia, trichomoniasis and *Mycoplasma genitalium*
- Report a suspected treatment failure to the health department

## While you wait

- Avoid all sex.
- Don't take leftover or online antibiotics.
- Make sure partners are tested and treated.
