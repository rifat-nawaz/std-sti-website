## Possible complications

| Complication | Who |
|---|---|
| PID, infertility, ectopic pregnancy | People with a uterus |
| Epididymitis, urethral scarring | People with a penis |
| Disseminated infection | Anyone, rarely |
| Eye damage | Anyone with eye infection |
| Higher HIV risk | Anyone |
