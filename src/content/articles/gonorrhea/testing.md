## Test types

| Test | Sample | Use |
|---|---|---|
| NAAT | Urine, vaginal, throat or rectal swab | Diagnosis; most accurate |
| Culture | Swab | Checks antibiotic resistance |
| Gram stain | Penile discharge | Quick clinic test in men with symptoms |

## 3-site testing

Test every site you've used for sex. A urine test won't detect throat or rectal infections.

## Where to test

Clinics, health departments (often free) and lab-processed home kits. See [best at-home gonorrhea tests](/gonorrhea/at-home-test/).
