## Why it may not mean cheating

- Silent cervical, throat or rectal infections can last months
- Earlier tests may have been too early or missed a site
- Many people have never been tested

## Next steps

Both get tested at every site and treated; avoid sex until finished. See [how to tell a partner](/guides/telling-a-partner/).
