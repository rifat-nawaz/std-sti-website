## Why infections happen with condoms

- Oral sex without a condom
- Condom put on partway through
- Breaks or slips
- Reusing condoms or using oil-based lube

You can also order a [home test kit](/go/k1/), collect your sample yourself and mail it to a certified lab.

## If a condom broke

1. Consider emergency contraception if pregnancy is possible.
2. Consider [PEP](/hiv/pep/) within 72 hours if HIV exposure is possible.
3. Test for gonorrhea and chlamydia at about 2 weeks.
