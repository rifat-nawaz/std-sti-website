## Your options

| Option | Speed | Cost | Privacy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Health department STD clinic | Often same day | Free or low | High |
| Urgent care | Same day | Insurance copay or cash price | Visit appears on insurance |
| Your doctor | Depends on appointments | Insurance | Visit appears on insurance |
| Planned Parenthood or community clinic | Often within days | Sliding scale | High |
| Telehealth | Fast advice | Varies | High, but usually needs an in-person follow-up |

Find clinics near you with the CDC's [GetTested](https://gettested.cdc.gov/) locator.

## What to bring

- Your positive test result, if you have one
- The dates of possible exposures
- A list of any medicines you take and allergies

## After your visit

Avoid sex for 7 days and until partners are treated. Read [getting treated for gonorrhea](/gonorrhea/treatment/).
