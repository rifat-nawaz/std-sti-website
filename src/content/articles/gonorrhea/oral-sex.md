## Who can pass it to whom

| Act | Possible infection |
|---|---|
| Giving oral sex to a penis | Throat |
| Receiving oral sex from a partner with throat gonorrhea | Penis |
| Oral sex on a vulva | Throat (less studied) |
| Rimming | Throat or rectum |

## Testing

Ask for a **throat swab**, about 2 weeks after exposure. See [throat gonorrhea](/gonorrhea/throat/).

For privacy and convenience, an [at-home test kit](/go/k1/) processed by a certified lab is another option.
