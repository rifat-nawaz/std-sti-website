## Common nicknames

| Slang | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The clap | Gonorrhea |
| The drip | Gonorrhea discharge from the penis |
| Gleet | Old term for ongoing discharge |

## Where "the clap" might come from

- **Old French "clapier"**, a word linked to brothels
- **Historical practices** of trying to clear discharge from the penis, which didn't work and caused harm

Neither origin is proven.

## Why it matters

If you think you have "the clap", ask for a gonorrhea and chlamydia test. See [how to test for gonorrhea](/gonorrhea/testing/).
