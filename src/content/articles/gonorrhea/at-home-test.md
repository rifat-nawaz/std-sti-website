## How we compare kits

We compare publicly available features: test type, samples, lab certification, FDA status, clinician support and privacy. We haven't lab-tested kits ourselves.

## Checklist

| Feature | Why |
|---|---|
| NAAT in a CLIA-certified lab | Clinic-level accuracy |
| Throat and rectal swabs | Needed after oral or anal sex |
| Chlamydia included | Same sample |
| Clinician follow-up | Helps you get treated fast |
| Fast shipping | Samples must reach the lab in time |

## FDA-authorized options

- Home sample collection for chlamydia and gonorrhea (2023)
- A results-at-home test for chlamydia, gonorrhea and trich, for people with a vagina (2025)
