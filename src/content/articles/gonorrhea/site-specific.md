## Sites and tests

| Site | Sample |
|---|---|
| Genitals | Urine or vaginal swab |
| Throat | Throat swab |
| Rectum | Rectal swab |

## Can it spread between sites?

Yes, through sex or touching, for example from the genitals to the rectum. That's why clinicians test every site you've used for sex.

## Which partners to tell

Anyone you've had sex with in the last 60 days, whatever the site.
