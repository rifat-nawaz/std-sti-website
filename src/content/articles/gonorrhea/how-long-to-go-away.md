## Typical timeline

| When | What to expect |
|---|---|
| 1 to 3 days | Discharge and burning ease |
| About 7 days | Most symptoms gone; safe to have sex if partners are treated |
| 7 to 14 days | Throat test of cure |
| 3 months | Retest for reinfection |

Symptoms continuing? See [still have symptoms after gonorrhea treatment?](/gonorrhea/symptoms-after-treatment/)
