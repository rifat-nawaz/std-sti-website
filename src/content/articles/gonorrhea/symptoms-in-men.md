## Common symptoms

| Symptom | Notes |
|---|---|
| Discharge | Thick, pus-like, white, yellow or green |
| Burning | During or after peeing |
| Testicle pain or swelling | Usually one side (epididymitis) |
| Rectal symptoms | After receptive anal sex |
| Sore throat | Uncommon |

## What to do

Get a urine NAAT, plus throat and rectal swabs if relevant. See [how to test for gonorrhea](/gonorrhea/testing/).

Prefer to test at home? A [lab-processed home test](/go/k1/) lets you collect a sample in private and get results online.
