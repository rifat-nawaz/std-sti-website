## Symptoms by site

| Site | Possible symptoms |
|---|---|
| Penis | Thick white, yellow or green discharge; burning; swollen testicle |
| Vagina or cervix | Discharge, burning, bleeding between periods, pelvic pain |
| Rectum | Discharge, itching, soreness, bleeding, painful bowel movements |
| Throat | Usually none; sometimes sore throat |
| Eye | Red, swollen eye with pus (urgent) |

## Urgent signs

- Fever with painful, swollen joints or skin spots
- Severe pelvic pain or sudden testicle pain
- Eye swelling and discharge
