## How it works

Gonorrhea bacteria attach to the moist lining of the urethra, cervix, rectum, throat or eyes, multiply and cause inflammation. In men this often causes noticeable discharge; in women and at other sites it's often silent.

If you'd rather test privately, a [lab-processed home kit](/go/k1/) uses the same type of lab test as a clinic.

## Body sites

| Site | Common symptoms |
|---|---|
| Penis | Discharge, burning |
| Cervix | Often none; discharge, bleeding |
| Rectum | Often none; pain, discharge |
| Throat | Usually none |
| Eyes | Red, swollen, pus-like discharge (urgent) |

## How serious is it?

Easy to cure when caught, but untreated gonorrhea can cause pelvic inflammatory disease, epididymitis, and rarely a dangerous blood and joint infection. See the [gonorrhea guide](/gonorrhea/).
