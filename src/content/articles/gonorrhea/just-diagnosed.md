## Checklist

| When | Do this |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Get seen; treatment is often given at the visit |
| Day 1 | Test for chlamydia, HIV and syphilis |
| Day 1 to 3 | Tell partners from the last 60 days |
| Until 7 days after treatment | No sex, including oral |
| Day 7 to 14 | Throat test of cure, if needed |
| 3 months | Retest |

See [getting treated for gonorrhea](/gonorrhea/treatment/).
