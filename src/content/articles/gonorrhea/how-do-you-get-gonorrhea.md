## Routes

| Route | Risk |
|---|---|
| Vaginal sex | High |
| Anal sex | High |
| Oral sex (either direction) | Moderate; throat infections are common |
| Rimming | Possible |
| Shared sex toys | Possible |
| Birth | Possible |
| Deep kissing | Under study; see [gonorrhea and kissing](/gonorrhea/kissing/) |

## Not a risk

Toilet seats, towels, swimming pools, hugging, shared dishes.
