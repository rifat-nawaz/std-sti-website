## Recommendations

| Who | How often |
|---|---|
| Sexually active women 24 and under | Yearly |
| Women 25+ at higher risk | Yearly |
| Pregnant people under 25 or at risk | First prenatal visit; again in third trimester if risk continues |
| Gay and bisexual men | At least yearly, all sites; every 3 to 6 months if at higher risk |
| People with HIV | At least yearly |
| Anyone treated for gonorrhea | Retest at about 3 months |
