## Risks of waiting

| Risk | Who |
|---|---|
| Pelvic inflammatory disease | People with a uterus |
| Epididymitis | People with testicles |
| Disseminated infection (joints, skin, blood) | Anyone, rarely |
| Higher HIV risk | Anyone |
| Passing it on | Partners |
