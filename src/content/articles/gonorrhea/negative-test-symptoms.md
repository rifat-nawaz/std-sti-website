## Tests to ask for

- Chlamydia NAAT
- Trichomoniasis NAAT
- *Mycoplasma genitalium* NAAT, if symptoms persist
- Urine culture for a UTI
- Throat and rectal swabs, if relevant

See [gonorrhea vs NGU](/gonorrhea/vs-ngu/) and [Mycoplasma genitalium](/mycoplasma-genitalium/).
