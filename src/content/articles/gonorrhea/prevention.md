## Prevention checklist

| Step | Why |
|---|---|
| Condoms for vaginal and anal sex | Block infected fluids |
| Condoms or dental dams for oral sex | Prevent throat infection |
| Regular testing, all sites | Finds silent infections |
| Partner treatment | Prevents reinfection |
| Limit shared toys or cover them | Prevent passing fluids |

See [who should be screened](/gonorrhea/screening/).
