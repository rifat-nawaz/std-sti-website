## Possible symptoms

- More vaginal discharge, sometimes yellowish
- Burning when peeing
- Bleeding between periods or after sex
- Lower belly or pelvic pain
- Rectal discomfort or discharge

## Mistaken for

| Condition | Clue it might be gonorrhea |
|---|---|
| UTI | Discharge, or no improvement after UTI treatment |
| Yeast infection | No itch, or no improvement with yeast products |
| BV | Bleeding or pelvic pain |

## What to do

Ask for a gonorrhea and chlamydia test, and trichomoniasis too. See [chlamydia and PID](/chlamydia/pid/).
