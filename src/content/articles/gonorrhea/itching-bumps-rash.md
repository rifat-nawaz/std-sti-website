## What different signs suggest

| Sign | More likely |
|---|---|
| Rectal itching and discharge | Rectal gonorrhea or chlamydia |
| Painful blisters | Herpes |
| Painless firm sore | Syphilis |
| Rash on palms and soles | Syphilis |
| Few skin spots plus fever and joint pain | Disseminated gonorrhea |
