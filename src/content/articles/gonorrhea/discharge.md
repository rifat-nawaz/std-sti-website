## How it compares

| Discharge | More likely cause |
|---|---|
| Thick yellow or green from the penis | Gonorrhea |
| Clear or cloudy, thin | Chlamydia, NGU |
| Frothy yellow-green, fishy | Trichomoniasis |
| Thin gray, fishy | BV |
| Thick white, clumpy | Yeast |
