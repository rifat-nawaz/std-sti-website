## Warning signs in a newborn

- Swollen, red eyelids
- Thick pus from the eyes
- Starting a few days after birth

Take your baby to a pediatrician or emergency room the same day.

## The eye ointment

A routine preventive ointment protects against gonorrhea eye infection. If you're considering declining it, talk to your provider about testing results and risks first.
