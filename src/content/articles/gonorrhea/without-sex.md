## No risk

- Toilet seats
- Towels and sheets
- Swimming pools, hot tubs, shared baths
- Hugging or casual kissing

For privacy and convenience, an [at-home test kit](/go/k1/) processed by a certified lab is another option.

## Some risk

- Genital-to-genital contact without penetration
- Shared sex toys
- Oral sex

## In children

Gonorrhea in a child beyond the newborn period is a strong sign of sexual contact. A clinician must assess the child, and suspected abuse must be reported. Call 911 if a child is in danger.
