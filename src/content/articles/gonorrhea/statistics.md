## Key numbers (CDC, 2023)

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Reported cases | 601,319 |
| Rate per 100,000 | 179.5 |
| Change from 2022 | Down 7.2% |
| Rank among reportable STDs | 2nd |

Prefer to test at home? A [lab-processed home test](/go/k1/) lets you collect a sample in private and get results online.

## Who is most affected

- **Young adults**, especially ages 20 to 29
- **Gay, bisexual and other men who have sex with men**
- Some racial and ethnic groups face higher rates, reflecting differences in access to testing and care, not behavior

## Worldwide

The WHO estimates about 82 million new gonorrhea infections worldwide in 2020.

Test yearly if you're at risk. See [who should be screened](/gonorrhea/screening/).
