## Symptoms

- Discharge or mucus
- Itching or soreness
- Bleeding
- Painful bowel movements
- Often nothing

## Testing

Self-collect a rectal swab about 1 to 1.5 inches inside and rotate gently. See [chlamydia sample collection](/chlamydia/sample-collection/) for step-by-step instructions that also apply to gonorrhea.

Prefer to test at home? A [lab-processed home test](/go/k1/) lets you collect a sample in private and get results online.

## Prevention

Condoms, regular testing and [PrEP](/hiv/prep/) for HIV protection.
