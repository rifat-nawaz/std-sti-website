## Symptoms and their likely causes

| Symptom | Possible STDs | Other possible causes |
|---|---|---|
| Discharge from the penis | Gonorrhea, chlamydia, trichomoniasis, *Mycoplasma genitalium* | Non-specific urethritis |
| Burning or pain when peeing | Same as above | Urinary tract or prostate infection |
| Painful blisters or sores | Herpes | Friction, ingrown hairs |
| Firm, painless sore | Syphilis | Rarely, other infections |
| Bumps or warts | HPV | Skin tags, pearly penile papules (harmless) |
| Testicle pain or swelling | Chlamydia, gonorrhea (epididymitis) | Testicular torsion (emergency), injury |
| Rectal pain, discharge or bleeding | Gonorrhea, chlamydia, herpes, syphilis | Hemorrhoids, fissures |
| Sore throat after oral sex | Gonorrhea, rarely chlamydia | Common colds and throat infections |
| Rash, especially on palms and soles | Secondary syphilis | Many skin conditions |
| Flu-like illness 2 to 4 weeks after sex | Acute HIV | Common viral infections |

## Symptoms by infection

- **Gonorrhea:** thick white, yellow or green discharge and burning, often within a week.
- **Chlamydia:** clear or cloudy discharge and mild burning, often absent.
- **Trichomoniasis:** usually no symptoms; sometimes itching or burning after peeing or ejaculating.
- **Herpes:** tingling, then blisters that break into painful sores.
- **Syphilis:** a painless sore, then weeks later a rash.
- **HPV:** painless warts, or nothing at all.

## What to do

1. **Don't self-treat** with leftover or online medicine. The wrong medicine can fail and drive antibiotic resistance.
2. **Get tested now if you have symptoms.** You don't need to wait for a window period.
3. **Get the right swabs.** Tell the clinic about oral and anal sex.
4. **Avoid sex** until you've been tested and, if needed, treated.
