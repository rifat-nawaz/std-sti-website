## Care you can ask for

| Care | Timing |
|---|---|
| HIV PEP | Within 72 hours |
| Emergency contraception | As soon as possible |
| Medicine to prevent common STDs | At the first visit |
| Hepatitis B vaccine | At the first visit if not protected |
| Forensic exam | Ideally within a few days |
| Follow-up STD and HIV tests | In the weeks and months after |

## Support

RAINN: **800-656-4673**. In crisis, call or text **988**.
