## How often each STD is silent

| Infection | Symptoms are... | Can still cause |
|---|---|---|
| Chlamydia | Absent in most people | PID, infertility, ectopic pregnancy |
| Gonorrhea | Often absent, especially in women, throat and rectum | PID, epididymitis, spread to joints |
| Trichomoniasis | Absent in about 70% | Higher HIV risk, pregnancy problems |
| HPV | Absent in most people | Warts, cervical and other cancers |
| Herpes | Mild or unnoticed in most people | Spread to partners, risk to newborns |
| HIV | Often absent for years after an early flu-like illness | Immune damage without treatment |
| Syphilis | Sore often painless and missed; latent stage has none | Brain, eye, heart damage; stillbirth |
| Hepatitis B and C | Usually absent for years | Liver scarring and cancer |

## Why silent infections matter

- They can still damage your body. Chlamydia can scar the fallopian tubes without causing pain.
- They spread to partners who don't know to test.
- They raise the risk of getting HIV.

## Who should test without symptoms

- Sexually active women 24 and under: chlamydia and gonorrhea every year
- Everyone 15 to 65: HIV at least once
- Every adult: hepatitis B and hepatitis C at least once
- Everyone who is pregnant: HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B, plus others based on risk
- Gay and bisexual men: HIV, syphilis, chlamydia and gonorrhea at least yearly, at every site used for sex
- Anyone with a new partner or more than one partner
