## Common symptoms and what they can mean

| Symptom | Possible STD causes | Possible non-STD causes |
|---|---|---|
| Discharge | Chlamydia, gonorrhea, trichomoniasis | BV, yeast infection |
| Burning when peeing | Chlamydia, gonorrhea, trichomoniasis, *Mycoplasma genitalium* | Urinary tract infection |
| Painful blisters or sores | Herpes | Friction, ingrown hair |
| Painless firm sore | Syphilis | Less often, other skin conditions |
| Bumps or warts | HPV (genital warts) | Skin tags, molluscum |
| Rash on palms or soles | Secondary syphilis | Many skin conditions |
| Flu-like illness weeks after sex | Early HIV, syphilis | Common viral infections |

## Why symptoms alone aren't enough

The same symptom can come from several infections, and you can have more than one at once. Most chlamydia, many gonorrhea and most HPV infections cause no symptoms at all.

That's why clinicians test rather than guess, and why a negative test with ongoing symptoms means going back for more testing, not ignoring it.
