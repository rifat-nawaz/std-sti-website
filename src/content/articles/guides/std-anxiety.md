## While you wait

- Know your test dates: [window period calculator](/std-testing/window-period/).
- Limit online symptom searching.
- Keep up normal routines: sleep, exercise, friends.

## After a diagnosis

Shame and guilt are common. STDs are infections, not judgments. Most people feel better once they have a plan.

## Get support

Counselors, support groups, and **988** for crisis support.
