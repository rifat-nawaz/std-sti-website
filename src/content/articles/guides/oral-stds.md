## Oral STDs at a glance

| Infection | Mouth signs | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Gonorrhea | Usually none; sore throat | Throat swab |
| Chlamydia | Usually none | Throat swab |
| Syphilis | Painless sore, white patches | Blood test |
| Herpes (HSV-1) | Cold sores | Swab of a sore |
| HPV | Warts; rarely throat cancer later | No routine test |
