## Prenatal STD tests

| Test | Who | When |
|---|---|---|
| HIV | Everyone | First visit; again later if at risk |
| Syphilis | Everyone | First visit; often 28 weeks and delivery |
| Hepatitis B | Everyone | Every pregnancy |
| Hepatitis C | Everyone | Every pregnancy |
| Chlamydia, gonorrhea | Under 25 or at risk | First visit; third trimester if at risk |
