## Testing by anatomy

| Anatomy used for sex | Sample |
|---|---|
| Penis | Urine |
| Vagina | Vaginal swab |
| Neovagina | Discuss with your clinician |
| Mouth | Throat swab |
| Anus | Rectal swab |

Prefer to test at home? A [lab-processed home test](/go/k1/) lets you collect a sample in private and get results online.

## Finding affirming care

LGBTQ+ community health centers, sexual health clinics and many telehealth services offer affirming STD testing.
