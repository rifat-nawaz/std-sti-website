## Symptoms and their likely causes

| Symptom | Possible STDs | Non-STD causes |
|---|---|---|
| Unusual discharge | Chlamydia, gonorrhea, trichomoniasis | BV, yeast infection |
| Fishy smell | Trichomoniasis | BV |
| Burning when peeing | Chlamydia, gonorrhea, trichomoniasis, herpes | Urinary tract infection |
| Bleeding between periods or after sex | Chlamydia, gonorrhea | Cervical polyps, hormonal changes |
| Pelvic pain or pain during sex | Chlamydia, gonorrhea (PID), *Mycoplasma genitalium* | Endometriosis, ovarian cysts |
| Painful blisters or sores | Herpes | Friction, ingrown hairs |
| Firm, painless sore | Syphilis | Rarely, other infections |
| Bumps or warts | HPV | Skin tags, molluscum |
| Itching | Trichomoniasis, herpes | Yeast infection, irritation |

## Pelvic inflammatory disease (PID)

When chlamydia or gonorrhea spread to the uterus and fallopian tubes, they cause PID. Signs include lower belly pain, pain during sex, fever and unusual bleeding.

PID needs prompt antibiotic treatment. Repeated or untreated PID raises the risk of infertility, ectopic pregnancy and long-term pelvic pain.

## STDs and pregnancy

Several STDs can affect a pregnancy or pass to the baby. The CDC recommends testing for HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B in every pregnancy, plus chlamydia and gonorrhea for people 24 and under or at higher risk.

## What to do

1. **Get tested now if you have symptoms.** A vaginal swab, which you can often collect yourself, is the most accurate sample for chlamydia, gonorrhea and trich.
2. **Ask for trich** if it isn't in the standard panel.
3. **Avoid douching** before testing. It can affect results.
4. **Avoid sex** until you've been tested and treated if needed.
