## Your toolkit

| Tool | Protects against |
|---|---|
| External or internal condoms | Most STDs, pregnancy |
| Dental dams | STDs during oral sex on a vulva or anus |
| HPV vaccine | HPV-related warts and cancers |
| Hepatitis B vaccine | Hepatitis B |
| PrEP | HIV |
| Regular testing | Finds silent infections |

Prefer to test at home? A [lab-processed home test](/go/k1/) lets you collect a sample in private and get results online.

## Sex toy hygiene

Wash with soap and water or as the maker instructs, and use a new condom when sharing or switching between anus and vagina.
