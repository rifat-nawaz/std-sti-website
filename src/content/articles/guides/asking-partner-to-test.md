## What to say

> "Before we have sex, I'd love for us both to get tested. I got tested last month and I'm happy to show you my results. Want to go together?"

You can also order a [home test kit](/go/k1/), collect your sample yourself and mail it to a certified lab.

## If they get defensive

Explain that most STDs have no symptoms, so testing is the only way either of you can know.
